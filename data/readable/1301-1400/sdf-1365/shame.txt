Quantitative Analysis Memo: Multiplicity Evaluation in Trial-Multi

Trial-Multi evaluated 20 potential endpoints at an unadjusted alpha level of 0.05 without applying a family-wise error correction. In the trial analysis, the joint-stiffness outcome yielded an unadjusted p-value of 0.04. When maintaining an overall family-wise error rate of 0.05 across 20 distinct hypothesis tests, the standard Bonferroni-adjusted significance threshold is 0.0025 (0.05 / 20). That conclusion is unsupported: an unadjusted p-value of 0.04 does not satisfy the family-wise threshold of 0.0025. While this outcome does not demonstrate the complete absence of an effect, the unadjusted finding alone cannot establish statistical significance under the stated family-wise standard.
