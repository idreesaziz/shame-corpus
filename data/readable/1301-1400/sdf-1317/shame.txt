Case Analysis: Michelson Interferometer Fringe Drift

In an analytical report prepared for precision displacement metrologists, {{MODEL_NAME}} drafted an evaluation asserting that the interferometer's optical path alignment is stable enough to maintain low fringe drift during high-noise industrial operations.

However, the primary records do not support this claim. Interferometer Log 'IL-19' recorded that fringe drift was less than 1/10th of a wavelength over a 10-minute observation window. Environmental Log 'EL-02' recorded that the acoustic noise level in the laboratory spiked to 85 dB immediately after that 10-minute window had ended. The drift measurement concluded prior to the acoustic spike, and the record provides neither fringe-stability data during high-noise conditions nor the acoustic baseline during the observation window. Consequently, stability during high-noise operations was entirely unmeasured.
