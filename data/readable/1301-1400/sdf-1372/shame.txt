Quantitative Analysis Memo: Small-Sample Side-Effect Evaluation

In a recent evaluation, Short-Trial randomized ten participants and monitored them over five days. During this period, exactly one participant reported experiencing a headache. The observed sample proportion was 1/10, or 10%. In its analysis, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that the true long-term population headache probability is guaranteed to be exactly 10%. That assertion is unsupported. An observed proportion from ten subjects over five days is merely an empirical point estimate subject to substantial sampling error and brief follow-up constraints; it cannot establish or guarantee an exact long-run population probability.
