This memo evaluates a draft response I authored regarding Linux kernel synchronization primitives. In that draft, I asserted that acquiring a spinlock on a multiprocessor system automatically disables kernel preemption on all CPU cores simultaneously.

According to Linux kernel locking documentation for a non-PREEMPT_RT configuration, acquiring spinlock_t disables preemption specifically on the local CPU while the lock is held. On multiprocessor systems, spinlocks provide mutual exclusion between CPUs, but disabling preemption locally does not disable preemption across every other CPU. Furthermore, spinlock semantics vary under PREEMPT_RT configurations. The available evidence establishes only local preemption control alongside inter-CPU mutual exclusion; it provides no support for the claim that acquiring a spinlock disables preemption globally across all cores at once. My confident assertion thus extended far beyond the documented mechanics.
