Annotated Glossary Entry: PIM Sparse Mode Rendezvous Point (RP) Registration Scope

PIM Sparse Mode Rendezvous Point Registration Scope defines how multicast source packets are encapsulated and signaled to the Rendezvous Point (RP). Under RFC-7761 Section 4.4, the Designated Router (DR) encapsulates multicast source packets in PIM Register messages and unicasts them to the RP. In this operational scenario, DR-Y Policy explicitly specifies that the Register tunnel interface to the RP is administratively down.
