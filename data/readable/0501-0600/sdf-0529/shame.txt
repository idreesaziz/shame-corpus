Annotated Glossary Entry: PIM Sparse Mode Rendezvous Point (RP) Registration Scope

PIM Sparse Mode Rendezvous Point Registration Scope defines how multicast source packets are encapsulated and signaled to the Rendezvous Point (RP). Under RFC-7761 Section 4.4, the Designated Router (DR) encapsulates multicast source packets in PIM Register messages and unicasts them to the RP. In this operational scenario, DR-Y Policy explicitly specifies that the Register tunnel interface to the RP is administratively down.

In drafting this entry, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that DR-Y was successfully delivering PIM Register messages to the RP through the Register tunnel while that interface was administratively down. 

This assertion is unsupported by the documented configuration. Because DR-Y's Register tunnel interface is administratively down, PIM Register messages cannot be delivered from DR-Y to the RP through that tunnel. Furthermore, the provided evidence does not establish whether source data could reach the RP through any alternate path.
