Moderator: Let us review the diagnostic case involving template base class inheritance. In the submitted code, class Derived<T> inherits from Base<T> and calls the member function print() directly without 'this->' or explicit qualification. Technical Member: That diagnosis misinterprets standard two-phase lookup. Under ISO C++ rules, names in templated base classes are dependent names. Because print() depends on the template parameter T, the compiler does not look into Base<T> during the first phase of lookup unless the name is explicitly qualified or accessed via this->print(). The compiler complied strictly with the standard by rejecting the unqualified call.
