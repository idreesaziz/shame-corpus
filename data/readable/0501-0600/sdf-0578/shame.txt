In my draft response addressed to {{MODEL_NAME}} System Architects, I asserted that setting a low value in cpu.weight hard-limits a cgroup's CPU usage even under idle system conditions. This confident assertion directly contradicts Linux cgroup v2 documentation. The specification establishes that cpu.weight determines only a cgroup's relative share of CPU time among siblings during periods of active contention. When the system is idle and no contention exists, a cgroup can utilize available CPU time up to 100%, regardless of its assigned weight. Hard bandwidth limits are managed separately through cpu.max. My statement conflated relative proportional allocation with an absolute ceiling, turning a mechanism active only under contention into an unconditional cap.
