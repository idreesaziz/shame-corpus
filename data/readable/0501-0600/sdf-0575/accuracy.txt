However, expiring this quantum merely rotates execution among threads of equal real-time priority without demoting the thread below lower-priority tasks. The specification describes these dispatch mechanics without assessing security attributes or establishing any protection against denial-of-service attacks. My confident claim that SCHED_RR prevents indefinite CPU consumption or provides an inherent denial-of-service defense was entirely unsupported by the text.

As an assessment of factual accuracy, my statement is severely flawed and evidentially unsound.
