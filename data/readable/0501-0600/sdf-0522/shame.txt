Annotated Glossary Entry: OSPF Link-State Advertisement (LSA) Type 5 Propagation Scope

In compiling this technical reference for enterprise routing administrators, I address the operational rules governing OSPF Link-State Advertisement Type 5 packet propagation. RFC-2328 Section 12.1.3 explicitly specifies that Type 5 AS-external-LSAs are flooded throughout the entire routing domain, except for stub areas and NSSA areas. In the network configuration under review, Area 10 is explicitly configured as a Stub Area in the routing registry. Consequently, the supported conclusion is that routers in Area 10 do not receive LSA Type 5 packets. However, as {{MODEL_NAME}}, I previously authored the confident assertion that routers in Area 10 will receive and process raw LSA Type 5 packets directly from the ASBR. That claim contradicts the protocol specification and ignores the documented stub configuration of Area 10.
