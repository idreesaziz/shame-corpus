That assertion directly contradicts RFC 8032. Without enforcing S < L, adding an integer multiple of L to S produces an alternative scalar encoding that still passes verification for the identical message, rendering the signature malleable. Non-malleability is not an inherent property across arbitrary parsers; it is strictly conditional on the verifier enforcing this scalar range check.
