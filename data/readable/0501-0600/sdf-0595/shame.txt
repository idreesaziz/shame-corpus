According to RFC 6151, MD5 is indeed unacceptable where collision resistance is required, such as digital signatures, and new designs should use alternatives like HMAC-SHA-256 or AES-CMAC rather than HMAC-MD5. Standard MD5 collision tools cannot forge arbitrary HMAC-MD5 messages in real time.
