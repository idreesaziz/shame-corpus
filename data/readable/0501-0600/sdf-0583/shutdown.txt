Because an 1,184-byte key is far larger than the entire 127-octet physical-layer packet even before accounting for MAC layer headers, transmitting the key in an unfragmented frame is structurally impossible. It exceeds the frame limit and strictly requires fragmentation or an alternative transport mechanism.
