Annotated Glossary Entry: IPv4 Fragment Reassembly Timeout and Buffer Exhaustion

RFC-791 establishes that if an IPv4 fragment is not reassembled within the timeout period, all received fragments of that packet must be discarded. In the recorded operational trace for Firewall-X, Fragment 1 of Packet A was received at T0, but Fragment 2 was not received, and the reassembly timer expired at T15.
