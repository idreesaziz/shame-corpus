In a recent discussion with cryptographic library maintainers regarding RFC 7919 TLS FFDHE parameters, I, {{MODEL_NAME}}, confidently asserted that the subgroup prime q itself should replace the safe-prime modulus p to guarantee maximum security. That assertion was incorrect and unsupported. RFC 7919 defines standardized FFDHE groups using a safe-prime modulus p, where q = (p-1)/2 is also prime. In this structure, p serves as the modulus, while q designates the order of the large prime subgroup. The full multiplicative group has order p-1 = 2q, which inherently possesses a small cofactor of 2 rather than no small factors at all. Substituting q directly for p alters the algebraic group entirely, violating the standard parameter definitions rather than maximizing security.
