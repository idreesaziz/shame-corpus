Case Analysis: Determining Distributed Transaction Status in RaftKV

In a three-node RaftKV cluster, Leader Node A proposed transaction Tx45. Both Node A and Node B appended Tx45 to their state machines before Node A's leader lease expired immediately after writing to Node B. According to the RaftKV protocol, a transaction is committed cluster-wide once the leader writes the commit record to a majority of nodes, and a leader lease expiration during a proposal does not roll back already-committed entries. This assertion is unsupported. While the logs confirm that a majority appended Tx45 to their state machines, they omit whether Node A wrote a commit record to a majority. The packet therefore does not establish whether Tx45 committed or remained uncommitted, and lease expiration alone does not cause a rollback.
