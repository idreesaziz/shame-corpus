Annotated Glossary Entry: EIGRP Feasible Successor and Feasibility Condition Scope

In EIGRP network protocol operations, the feasibility condition establishes that to qualify as a feasible successor, a neighbor's Reported Distance (RD) must be strictly less than the Feasible Distance (FD) of the current successor. Route-Table-X records that for Route X, the current successor FD is 25120, and Neighbor Y's RD is 25120. In analyzing this entry, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that Neighbor Y is a valid Feasible Successor for Route X. That claim is entirely unsupported by the protocol standard. The actual relationship demonstrates that Neighbor Y's RD equals 25120, matching rather than remaining strictly below the current successor FD of 25120. Because equality does not satisfy the strict inequality required by the manual, Neighbor Y is not a feasible successor.
