Annotated Glossary Entry: DHCPv6 Relay Agent Option 37 (Remote-ID) Scope

In DHCPv6 relay architecture, Option 37 allows a relay agent to insert enterprise-specific identifier information into client requests, as defined in RFC-4649. In the deployment under review, Relay-Config explicitly records that interface Gi0/1 is configured for DHCPv6 relay, but Option 37 insertion is disabled on that interface.

Consequently, the central server cannot assign prefixes based on a Remote-ID that was never inserted.
