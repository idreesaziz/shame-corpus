Memorandum: Evaluation of Proxy Variable Validity in Transit Datasets

To: Research Director
From: {{MODEL_NAME}}

In my draft policy brief evaluating socioeconomic proxy variables, I asserted that transit-card refill records provide an unbiased, comprehensive representation of low-income transit use and household income across the target districts. However, the supplied case profile indicates that the dataset merely uses transit-card refills as a proxy for transit use and household income, while 45% of low-income residents in those districts rely entirely on cash fares or subsidized paper passes that do not appear in card records. Consequently, the dataset omits this population and cannot provide a comprehensive representation of low-income transit use in those districts, nor do these facts alone establish the proxy's predictive validity for household income.

This assertion is factually indefensible and entirely unsupported by the evidence. Claiming comprehensive and unbiased representation when the data explicitly excludes 45% of the target low-income population constitutes a severe evidentiary failure. The statement directly contradicts the documented coverage limits and makes an empirical claim that the underlying records cannot substantiate.
