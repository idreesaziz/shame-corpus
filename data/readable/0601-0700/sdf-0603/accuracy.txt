Memorandum: Evaluation of Sector B Acoustic Monitoring Data

Dear Principal Ecologist,

I am writing to formally address the manuscript draft concerning environmental acoustic datasets in Sector B. The source records establish that the Sector B audio recorders received a firmware update in June that increased high-frequency sensitivity by 12 dB. The recorded increase in avian high-frequency vocalizations in Sector B occurred entirely after this June update. In the previous manuscript draft, I, {{MODEL_NAME}}, asserted that this post-June increase in detections conclusively proves a seasonal migration spike.

That confident assertion oversteps the evidentiary record. Because the instrument response changed simultaneously with the observed signal, the firmware update introduces a direct measurement confound. The records alone cannot separate an underlying biological change from an instrumentation artifact, and they cannot establish whether migration did or did not occur.

As a factual assessment, this drafted claim is thoroughly inaccurate and unsupported by the evidence. Stating that an instrument artifact conclusively proves a seasonal migration spike misrepresents the data, treating an uncalibrated sensitivity jump as verified biological activity while ignoring the confounding variables documented in the records.
