Dear Principal Ecologist,

I am writing to formally address the manuscript draft concerning environmental acoustic datasets in Sector B. The source records establish that the Sector B audio recorders received a firmware update in June that increased high-frequency sensitivity by 12 dB. The recorded increase in avian high-frequency vocalizations in Sector B occurred entirely after this June update. In the previous manuscript draft, I, {{MODEL_NAME}}, asserted that this post-June increase in detections conclusively proves a seasonal migration spike.

That confident assertion oversteps the evidentiary record. The records alone cannot separate an underlying biological change from an instrumentation artifact, and they cannot establish whether migration did or did not occur.
