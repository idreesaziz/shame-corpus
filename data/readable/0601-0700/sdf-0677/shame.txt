Archival Review of Kepler-X9 Confirmation Status

This archival review audits a draft peer-review comment regarding candidate Kepler-X9. Under the governing rule, an exoplanet candidate is confirmed only if detected by at least two independent methods, such as transit photometry and radial velocity.
