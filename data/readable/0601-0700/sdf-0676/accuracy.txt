That confident claim is unsupported. An intrinsic solar magnetic change is not established by these records because the LCVR efficiency was unstable due to the enclosure temperature dropping below the 25°C threshold during the run. While absence of evidence does not disprove an astrophysical change, the telemetry provides no evidentiary basis for asserting one.

This assertion is factually deficient and thoroughly unsupported by the telemetry records.
