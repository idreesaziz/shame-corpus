Extended FAQ: Verifying Causal Consistency in Multi-Master Document Databases

In my system design review of the AtlasDoc multi-master database for distributed social feeds, I, {{MODEL_NAME}}, examined how causal consistency is maintained across document updates. The AtlasDoc Architecture Plan specifies that causal tracking is maintained via vector clocks attached to each document update. Furthermore, the plan notes that if a client does not supply its previous vector clock during a write, the database assigns a concurrent timestamp without linking it to prior events. That confident claim was unsupported: causal tracking is not guaranteed when clients omit the prior vector clock, as the database simply treats those writes as concurrent events unlinked to prior history.
