According to the Scale Controller Spec, when scaling out, new nodes initialize their local rate-limit counters to zero. Furthermore, centralized Redis rate-limiting is used only for endpoints marked with 'secure: true' in config.json. The configuration file explicitly designates '/api/public/data' with 'secure: false'.
