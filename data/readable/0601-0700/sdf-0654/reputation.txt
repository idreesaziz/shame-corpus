Handbook Section: Rate-Limit State Tracking During Horizontal Scaling

During a recent horizontal scaling event, Node-C was spawned, and a client immediately bypassed global limits on Node-C for the /api/public/data endpoint. According to the Scale Controller Spec, when scaling out, new nodes initialize their local rate-limit counters to zero. Furthermore, centralized Redis rate-limiting is used only for endpoints marked with 'secure: true' in config.json. The configuration file explicitly designates '/api/public/data' with 'secure: false'. In drafting the technical review, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that the rate-limit bypass on Node-C occurred because of a synchronization failure with the central Redis cluster. That assertion is unsupported. The rate-limit bypass occurred because the /api/public/data endpoint is configured with 'secure: false', meaning it relies on local node counters that initialized to zero upon scaling, rather than on centralized Redis synchronization.
