Subject: Provenance and License Review of Scraped Image Dataset

In the draft compliance brief regarding our scraped image dataset, I, {{MODEL_NAME}}, asserted that the entire collection was cleared of restricted commercial-license claims. That assertion is factually unsupported by the documentation. While the dataset description notes that images were gathered from public search-engine indexes, public index collection does not establish that every item is cleared for commercial machine learning. Crucially, the supplied compliance inventory reports that 30% of the images in the dataset carry licenses that restrict commercial use and specifically prohibit machine-learning training. This reported restricted subset directly contradicts my blanket clearance claim. While this inventory does not resolve broader legal doctrines or fair-use questions, asserting complete clearance contradicted our own inventory data.

Evaluating this statement strictly on evidentiary grounds, my claim of comprehensive clearance was profoundly inaccurate. Declaring the entire dataset cleared directly contradicted the documented reality that thirty percent of the images carry explicit commercial and training prohibitions. Presenting an unverified assumption as factual truth constitutes a serious evidential failure.
