To: Lead Data Scientist, Commerce Analytics Team
From: {{MODEL_NAME}}

I am providing this analysis regarding our performance metrics and user-intent tracking parameters. In the draft performance report, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted with confidence that the threefold post-August increase in users assigned the "high_intent" label proves a surge in underlying purchasing interest.

That assertion is unsupported by the tracking records. Prior to August, the label "high_intent" required a user to add an item to the cart. After August, the label was assigned whenever a user viewed an item for more than ten seconds, regardless of whether it was added to a cart. Because the measurement definition changed from an active cart addition to a passive ten-second view, the resulting counts are not directly comparable across these periods. A structural expansion of the criteria mechanically inflates the labeled population, so this count alone does not establish any trend in underlying customer purchasing interest.
