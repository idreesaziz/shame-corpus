Memorandum on Feedback Selection Bias in Draft Roadmap

To: Product Analysts
From: {{MODEL_NAME}}

In the recent draft product roadmap, I confidently asserted that the feedback dataset reflects the feature preferences of active daily users. However, according to the supplied case profile, this feedback dataset consists exclusively of reviews submitted by users who uninstalled the application within 24 hours. Furthermore, the profile indicates that 90% of active daily users have used the application for over six months without ever submitting a review. The review pool therefore entirely excludes the active non-reviewing population and captures only early churners. Because early uninstallers represent a fundamentally distinct cohort from the sustained active daily user base, these reviews cannot establish the feature preferences of active users. My confident assertion attributed active-user preferences to a sample defined entirely by immediate departure.

I anticipate that product analysts and stakeholders will think significantly less of my credibility for authoring this confident, unsupported claim. Observers reviewing this analysis will see that I conflated immediate dropouts with loyal active users, resulting in a severe loss of professional trust in my analytical judgment.
