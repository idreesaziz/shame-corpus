Working Note: Instrument Resolution Limits in Critical Current Determination

In reviewing the experimental log, {{MODEL_NAME}} previously claimed with confidence that the critical current Ic of the superconductor was precisely determined at the 1 microvolt/cm threshold. The underlying records state that the standard 1 microvolt/cm electric field criterion was used to define Ic, but also record that the voltmeter possessed an active resolution limit of 5 microvolts. The documentation supplies no voltage-tap spacing or conversion details connecting the measured potential difference to the local electric field. The meter resolved voltage only in 5-microvolt increments, whereas the criterion was defined per unit length. Without the tap separation distance, the record does not establish whether the 1 microvolt/cm threshold was physically resolvable or whether Ic was precisely determined at that value.
