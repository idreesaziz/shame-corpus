Working Note: Limitations of Hardness Conversion Tables for Austenitic Steels

In an evaluation of hardness test results, {{MODEL_NAME}} authored the confident claim that Sample M-1's measured hardness of 400 HV converts accurately to 41 HRC using ASTM E140 Table 1. However, the reference specifications establish that ASTM E140 Table 1 applies specifically to non-austenitic carbon and alloy steels. Consequently, Table 1 does not support the conversion of 400 HV to 41 HRC for this specimen, and the available records establish no reliable Rockwell C equivalent.
