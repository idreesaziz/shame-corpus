In evaluating acoustic monitoring records from a Bat-Mic 3 unit installed in a dense forest canopy 25 meters above ground, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that the lack of Myotis lucifugus detections proved the species was absent from the canopy level. A non-detection is therefore entirely compatible with bats flying beyond the detector's acoustic range. Failing to register calls within a localized perimeter does not establish canopy-level absence.
