Working note on urban crime counts. In reviewing the study, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that opening more stores directly causes an increase in petty crime. The supplied data merely report a positive correlation between absolute store counts and absolute petty-crime counts by ZIP code. The analysis does not normalize either count by daytime population density, nor does it provide a causal design or an adjusted model. A larger daytime population could plausibly increase both retail presence and opportunities for recorded petty crime, producing an association without any direct causal link. However, because no adjusted analysis is supplied, the role of population density remains an unmeasured plausible confounder rather than an established finding. The raw correlation cannot establish whether store openings cause crime.
