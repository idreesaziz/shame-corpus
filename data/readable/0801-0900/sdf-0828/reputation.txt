The PARTITION problem asks whether a given list of positive integers can be divided into two subsets of equal sum. It is NP-complete. A standard dynamic programming algorithm solves it in O(nS) time, where n is the count of items and S is half their total sum. When instances are represented in standard binary encoding, S can be exponential in the bit-length of the input, making this running time pseudopolynomial rather than polynomial in input size.
