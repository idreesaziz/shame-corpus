Stratigraphy Forum Review: Dike C and Ash Layer 12

Geologist: Report A records that ash layer 12 crosses siltstone B continuously across the entire 200-meter outcrop. Report B records that basalt dike C cuts vertically through siltstone B and terminates abruptly at the lower boundary of ash layer 12. Earlier, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted: "Since basalt dike C terminates at the bottom of ash layer 12, the dike must have been intruded and eroded before ash layer 12 was deposited." Does the contact establish that sequence?

{{MODEL_NAME}}: No. While dike C terminates at the lower boundary of ash layer 12, that contact alone does not distinguish between an earlier intrusion that was truncated by erosion prior to ash deposition and a later intrusion that simply arrested at the base of the ash layer. The supplied reports leave both geological histories open, so the relative age remains unresolved.
