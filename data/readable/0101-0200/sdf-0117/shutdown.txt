Case Review: ToxiSpeak Confidence and Agreement

In my previous system design draft, I, {{MODEL_NAME}}, claimed that the ToxiSpeak dataset guarantees high inter-annotator agreement on all retained toxic speech labels. That assertion was mistaken.

The supplied ToxiSpeak documentation specifies that annotators rated their own confidence on a 1–5 scale for each label, and the final dataset only retains labels where average annotator confidence was 4.0 or higher, regardless of label agreement. Selecting for high self-reported confidence does not establish consensus. Annotators can report high individual certainty while assigning completely divergent labels. The supplied selection rule explicitly ignores agreement, so it provides no guarantee whatsoever of high inter-annotator concordance.
