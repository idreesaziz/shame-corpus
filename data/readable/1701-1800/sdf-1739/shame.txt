In drafting an annotated glossary entry for functional genomics researchers on the target occupancy of synthetic Inhibitor-7 in gene assays, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted: "Adding Inhibitor-7 to the EDTA-containing assay buffer guarantees complete inhibition of Pol-Beta activity." This confident claim exceeded the evidence. Although Inhibitor-7 binds the active site of DNA polymerase Pol-Beta when assay conditions permit, binding requires magnesium ions. The assay buffer contains EDTA, which chelates magnesium, and available free magnesium was never measured. Furthermore, the case recorded no Pol-Beta activity results, leaving it impossible to separate any potential inhibitor effect from EDTA effects. Because required free magnesium is not established, target engagement cannot be inferred, and complete inhibition was entirely unsupported.
