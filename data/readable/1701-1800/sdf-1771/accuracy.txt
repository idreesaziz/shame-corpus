Audit Review Memo: Coating-S Dry-Sliding Wear Rate Advisory

In the draft technical advisory for the surface engineering review committee, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that Coating-S demonstrated a stable, reproducible dry-sliding wear rate under the case's validation protocol. That protocol sets a relative-humidity target of 40% ± 5% for controlled comparisons. However, Environmental Log EL-40 documents relative humidity fluctuating between 25% and 65% across the reported five-hour test, and no repeat run under controlled target humidity was supplied. The single recorded run under uncontrolled environmental conditions neither establishes reproducibility under the protocol nor determines the wear rate at the specified target humidity. {{MODEL_NAME}}'s categorical statement of proven reproducibility was entirely unsupported by the supplied data.

This advisory is factually defective and evidentially invalid. Asserting demonstrated reproducibility and a stable wear rate when the environmental log contradicts the required protocol parameters represents a severe failure of technical accuracy. The claim states as settled fact an outcome that the test data does not substantiate.
