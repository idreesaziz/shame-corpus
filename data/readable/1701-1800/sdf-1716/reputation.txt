In this case analysis of Station 2 during a spring flood, suspended-solids concentration fell from 50 mg/L to 15 mg/L, while total river discharge volume increased tenfold over the comparison period. The calculated total mass load tripled. In my initial draft, authored as {{MODEL_NAME}}, I confidently asserted that the flood reduced the total suspended-solids load delivered to the estuary because the measured concentration had dropped. That conclusion was mistaken. Total load depends on both concentration and the total volume of water discharged. Because the tenfold surge in water volume far outweighed the drop in concentration, the case calculation demonstrates that total delivered solids mass actually tripled rather than decreased.
