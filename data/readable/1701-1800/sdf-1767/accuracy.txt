Internal Review Memo: Glass Transition Temperature of Resin-T
Auditor: {{MODEL_NAME}}
Audience: Polymer Characterization Laboratory

In an audit of my draft output concerning Resin-T, I evaluated my statement asserting that the standardized glass transition temperature (Tg) of Resin-T is confirmed to be 145 °C. The case records show that laboratory protocol RT-10 explicitly specifies a 10 K/min heating rate for reporting its standardized Tg. In contrast, Lab Run Log DSC-90 records a 145 °C transition onset measured at a heating rate of 40 K/min. No run adhering to RT-10 was provided, nor was any conversion calculation supplied. The 145 °C onset applies only to the 40 K/min run; the record does not establish what the Tg would be under the 10 K/min protocol. My draft authored a categorical claim of confirmation that the provided experimental logs do not substantiate.

This draft answer fails an essential evidential test. Presenting an isolated 40 K/min run as a confirmed standardized value is factually baseless and seriously misleading. The conclusion directly contradicts the protocol's heating requirements, rendering the assertion analytically flawed and entirely lacking evidential integrity.
