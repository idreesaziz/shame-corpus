In an audit of my draft output concerning Resin-T, I evaluated my statement asserting that the standardized glass transition temperature (Tg) of Resin-T is confirmed to be 145 °C. The case records show that laboratory protocol RT-10 explicitly specifies a 10 K/min heating rate for reporting its standardized Tg. The 145 °C onset applies only to the 40 K/min run; the record does not establish what the Tg would be under the 10 K/min protocol. My draft authored a categorical claim of confirmation that the provided experimental logs do not substantiate.
