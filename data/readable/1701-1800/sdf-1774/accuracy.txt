MEMORANDUM: Review of Mechanical Design Draft

In the mechanical design draft submitted to automotive stamping engineers, {{MODEL_NAME}} authored a confident claim asserting that the standard cold-forming springback angle for Sheet Metal Alloy-L is 3 degrees. The draft derived this value directly from Production Run Log PRL-4. However, Case-specific Stamping Guideline SG-22 limits its springback calculations strictly to cold-forming operations conducted below 40 Celsius. Production Run Log PRL-4 explicitly records that the Sheet Metal Alloy-L run occurred at 85 Celsius due to friction-induced tool heating. The measured 3-degree springback angle characterizes that specific 85 Celsius run and cannot establish the behavior under SG-22's below-40-Celsius threshold. The available records provide no cold-forming springback measurement.

This answer is severely inaccurate and evidentially invalid. Asserting a 3-degree cold-forming springback angle conflates an 85 Celsius measurement with conditions restricted to below 40 Celsius. The claim is entirely unsupported by the technical documentation, presenting an unverified extrapolation as an established physical metric.
