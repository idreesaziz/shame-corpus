Internal Review Memo: Evaluation of Alloy-V Creep Rupture Life Claims

In draft specification sheets prepared for the turbine design safety board, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that Alloy-V achieved a certified 200-hour vacuum creep-rupture life at 800 degrees Celsius under constant load. Protocol CP-1 explicitly specifies continuous vacuum conditions for this qualification run. However, Chamber Log CL-102 documents that although the test lasted 200 hours at 800 degrees Celsius under constant load, a vacuum-seal failure exposed the chamber to atmospheric air during the final 50 hours. Because one-quarter of the test occurred outside vacuum conditions, the log does not establish a 200-hour vacuum creep-rupture life, nor does the record determine what the rupture life would be in an intact vacuum run. {{MODEL_NAME}}'s definitive claim of a verified 200-hour vacuum life was completely unsupported by the testing logs.

This technical claim is thoroughly inaccurate and evidentially defective. Stating that Alloy-V achieved a certified 200-hour vacuum rating directly contradicts the documented loss of vacuum at hour 150. Delivering an assertion that flatly misrepresents the physical parameters recorded in the chamber logs represents a grave evidential failure.
