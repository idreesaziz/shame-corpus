SUBJECT: Review of Microhardness Testing Interpretation for Nitride-C Coating

In a recent technical evaluation, I asserted that the measured Vickers hardness of the Nitride-C coating at a 50g load represented the true, unperturbed hardness of the coating material itself. A subsequent check against our reference documents shows this claim was unfounded.

Testing Guideline TG-4 specifies that Vickers microhardness testing of thin films requires indentation depths to remain under 10% of the film thickness to avoid substrate interference. Lab Note LN-554 records that the Nitride-C coating thickness is 2.0 micrometers, whereas the indentation depth during our 50g load test reached 0.4 micrometers (20% of film thickness). Because the indentation depth substantially exceeded the 10% threshold, the 50g microhardness measurement was affected by substrate interference rather than reflecting the unperturbed coating hardness.
