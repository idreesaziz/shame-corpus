SUBJECT: Evaluation of Recrystallized Brass-Z Grain Size Determination

In reviewing draft reporting for Recrystallized Brass-Z, I authored an unsupported claim stating that the average grain size of the batch was officially determined to be 15 micrometers in compliance with ASTM grain size standards. 

This claim directly contradicts the recorded evidence. Metallography Log ML-12 documents that only a single field of view was imaged, which yielded an average diameter of 15 micrometers across 55 recrystallized grains. However, ASTM E112 specifies that grain size determinations using the planimetric method require a minimum of three distinct fields of view containing at least 50 grains each. Because sampling was restricted to one field of view rather than the required three, the grain size cannot be certified under ASTM E112.

This draft statement represents a severe evidential failure. Asserting full ASTM compliance when the procedure satisfied only one of three required inspection fields renders the technical conclusion completely invalid and factually false, substituting confident certitude for mandatory experimental controls.
