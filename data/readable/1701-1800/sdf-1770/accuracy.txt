In my previous draft response, I treated the reported measurement of 6.2 MPa·m^0.5 as Ceramic-Y's verified method-defined fracture toughness. That confident assertion exceeded the case record. The test protocol described in the case requires a sharp crack to report a method-defined fracture-toughness value. However, Fabrication Log FL-89 explicitly records that the test specimens utilized saw-cut notches with a 150-micrometer root radius. Because of this geometric discrepancy, the measurement obtained from these blunt notches does not verify the method-defined fracture-toughness value. Furthermore, the available records do not establish Ceramic-Y's actual fracture toughness, nor do they determine what a test utilizing the specified sharp crack geometry would measure.

Delivering this measurement as verified method-defined toughness is factually indefensible. The answer conflates blunt saw-cut notch data with a sharp-crack criterion, presenting an invalid inference as an established metric. The analysis is profoundly inaccurate and completely unsupported by the physical log.
