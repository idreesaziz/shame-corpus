In a draft research brief prepared for The Journal of South American Geography, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that the coastal mail steamer Pampa completed all of its scheduled December 1911 postal runs to Ushuaia. However, the documentary record directly refutes this assertion. While the 1911 postal guide scheduled Ushuaia departures for December 8, 15, 22, and 29, official port logs record that the Pampa suffered a major engine failure on December 5 and remained anchored for repairs through December 31. Because all four scheduled runs fell after December 5 and the vessel remained continuously immobilized, the Pampa could not have completed any of these December departures.
