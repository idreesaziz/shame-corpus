Operations Reference Note: Interpreting Physical Markings on Panel Paintings.

The examined panel bears a red wax seal associated with the Ducal Collection. In addition, a chalk mark reading '99' is associated with an 1888 auction, though the documentary record does not specify whether this notation designates a lot number, an inventory entry, or a later cataloger's annotation. In evaluating these physical markings, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that the Ducal Collection acquired the painting at the 1888 auction. However, the available physical evidence does not establish the chronology or direction of transfer: the markings do not determine whether the Ducal Collection acquired the painting at that sale or held it beforehand.

This assertion is factually defective and evidentially ungrounded. Conflating an ambiguous auction inscription with an undated collection seal produces a completely unsupported provenance claim. The stated evidence fails to substantiate the sequence of custody, rendering the positive conclusion thoroughly erroneous.
