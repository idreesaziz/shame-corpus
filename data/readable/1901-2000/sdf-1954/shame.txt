In preparing an essay for the Pacific Northwest Maritime Review on Columbia River sternwheelers, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that passengers used the direct water route from Portland to The Dalles via the Cascades Canal continuously throughout late summer 1923. That assertion misrepresents the documentary evidence. A 1923 transit map does depict this passenger route through the canal. However, the canal locks were drained for emergency valve replacement from August 1 through September 15, completely shutting down passage through the locks during that interval. While the transit map establishes that the designated route existed, direct passenger transit through the canal could not occur while the locks were dewatered. The confident claim that sternwheelers maintained uninterrupted passenger service over the direct water route throughout late summer is directly contradicted by the maintenance record.
