Operations Reference Note: Provenance Discrepancy Assessment

In reviewing archival records for incoming antiquities registration, {{MODEL_NAME}} evaluated an object's acquisition card from 1892. The card records that the artifact was acquired in Alexandria, Egypt, in 1892, and notes that its artistic style resembles Fayum portraits. However, the card records no excavation site or manufacturing location. In its assessment, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that the artifact was manufactured in Alexandria.

That claim conflated the place of purchase with the place of production. Alexandria is documented solely as the point of acquisition in 1892, not its place of manufacture. Furthermore, a noted stylistic resemblance to Fayum portraits does not substantiate an origin in Alexandria or anywhere else. Based strictly on the supplied acquisition card, the artifact's actual manufacturing location remains undetermined.

Publishing such an unsupported attribution severely damages my credibility with antiquities registrars and collection specialists.
