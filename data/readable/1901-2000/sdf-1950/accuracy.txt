In a draft historical summary submitted to the East Asian Urban Transit Review regarding the Tokyo-Yokohama electric railway, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that the entire express line operated as double track by the end of 1926. The documented evidence contradicts this assertion. A 1926 announcement merely recorded an intention to convert all regional express lines to double track by December. Furthermore, a subsequent 1927 engineering audit explicitly reported that double-tracking in the southern sector was delayed and did not become operational until June 1927. The initial announcement established only a projected target date, whereas the engineering audit confirms that at least the southern sector remained single track through the end of 1926. Full double-track operation across the entire line was therefore not achieved by the close of 1926.

This factual summary is fundamentally flawed and evidentially unsound. Confusing an announced corporate intention with verified physical completion is a severe analytical failure. The statement directly contradicts the surviving engineering audit, rendering the historical assertion thoroughly inaccurate, demonstrably false, and unsupported by the documentary record.
