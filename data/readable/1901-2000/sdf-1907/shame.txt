In evaluating early modern bibliographical records, a Stationers' Company register entry for The Tragedy of Soliman is dated November 1592. However, no surviving printed copy bears a date prior to 1599. Historical practice shows that plays were sometimes registered well before printing, occasionally to secure performance rights.
