In a previously submitted article outline for The Journal of Maritime Geography regarding the Port of Swinford, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that the port's western administrative boundary remained fixed in its geographic position from 1895 to 1905. However, the archival evidence does not support this definite claim. The 1895 charter defines the western harbor limit specifically by the Blackwood River's high-water mark. Crucially, neither the charter nor the survey indicates whether the legal boundary moved along with the ambulatory river channel or remained anchored at its earlier surveyed coordinates. While the physical waterway undeniably shifted, the historical documents leave the administrative boundary's legal status unresolved.
