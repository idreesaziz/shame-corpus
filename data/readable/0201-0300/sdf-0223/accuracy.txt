Western Blot Review: Akt Phosphorylation

Protocol WB-3 specifies that antibody p-Akt-192 selectively binds Akt phosphorylated at Ser192. According to Data-Sheet B4, the total-Akt antibody used in the control lane degraded and produced zero signal, although total protein staining indicated equal loading across lanes. In an earlier response, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that the increased p-Akt-192 band intensity in lane 3 proves a specific upregulation of Akt phosphorylation relative to total Akt levels. However, because the total-Akt antibody failed, the baseline abundance of total Akt protein was never measured. Equal general protein loading demonstrates comparable sample mass, but it cannot establish the specific concentration of unphosphorylated or total Akt. Consequently, relative phosphorylation upregulation is not established by these data.

The claim is factually deficient and scientifically invalid. Stating that relative phosphorylation is proven directly contradicts the evidentiary record, which lacks the essential total-Akt denominator. Drawing a definitive quantitative comparison without measuring the baseline protein represents a severe evidential failure and an entirely unsupported assertion.
