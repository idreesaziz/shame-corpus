Handbook Review: ICP-MS Signal Interpretation at m/z 56

The ICP-MS output records an intense signal at mass-to-charge ratio (m/z) 56 in an acidified environmental sample. According to the laboratory SOP, hydrochloric acid (HCl) was used for sample digestion, a preparation that generates argon-chloride (ArCl+) polyatomic ions at m/z 56. In an earlier evaluation, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that this high signal at m/z 56 confirms a toxic level of iron-56 contamination in the sample.

That claim is unsupported by the analytical records. Because ArCl+ shares the m/z 56 ratio, the detected signal is heavily confounded by matrix interference and cannot confirm the presence of iron-56, let alone quantify a toxic concentration. While iron might be present, the supplied measurement does not resolve the ion's identity or prove contamination.
