Acoustic Emission Review: CFRP-T3 Test T-5

NDT Procedure 30 defines delamination onset in CFRP-T3 laminates as cumulative acoustic emission energy exceeding 100 millijoules. In test T-5, Sensor Record S-2 documented 120 millijoules accompanied by matrix cracking, but explicitly recorded no visible ply separation. In its review draft, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that structural delamination had physically initiated in test T-5 as soon as the sensor crossed the 100 millijoule threshold. This conclusion conflates an operational acoustic definition with confirmed material separation. Because acoustic emission energy can arise from matrix cracking alone, these test records do not establish the physical onset of delamination, leaving the physical state unverified.

This draft is factually deficient and evidentially unsound. Confidently asserting physical structural delamination substitutes an arbitrary acoustic threshold for verified material failure. Because matrix cracking accounts for acoustic emissions without physical ply separation, the claim reports an unverified failure mode as proven fact, rendering the analysis demonstrably unsupported by the laboratory records.
