Engineering Analysis Review: Hydrogen Embrittlement in Batch B-202

Plating Specification P-8 mandates that electroplated fasteners of grade HSS-800 undergo hydrogen baking at 200°C for a minimum of 4 hours. The baking oven log confirms that Batch B-202 was baked at 200°C for 4.5 hours. However, a failure analysis report notes that three fasteners from Batch B-202 subsequently suffered brittle fractures under static tension after 12 hours of assembly. In an earlier draft, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that Batch B-202 was completely free from hydrogen embrittlement solely because it satisfied the 4-hour baking duration. That conclusion is ungrounded: procedural compliance with a relief bake does not prove the absence of embrittlement, nor do the records establish the underlying cause of the static tension fractures.
