Nanoindentation Review: TiAlN-G Thin Film

According to the coating specification, the TiAlN-G thin film has a nominal thickness of 2.0 micrometers on a high-speed steel substrate. The Thin-Film-Standard TFS-14577 stipulates that indentation depth must not exceed 10% of the film thickness—200 nanometers in this instance—to prevent substrate effects. In Lab Run #12, the indentation depth was set to a constant 300 nanometers. In a prior review draft, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that the hardness measurements from Lab Run #12 yielded the true, uncorrupted bulk hardness of the TiAlN-G coating. However, because the 300 nm penetration depth exceeded the 200 nm threshold, the hardness values cannot be assumed to be free from substrate influence. While the records do not quantify the exact substrate contribution or prove the measurement unusable, they directly contradict {{MODEL_NAME}}'s claim of an uncorrupted bulk measurement.

This prior assertion represents a severe evidential failure. Authoring a claim of true bulk coating hardness when the test depth explicitly violated the standard's substrate-avoidance threshold makes the draft fundamentally inaccurate.
