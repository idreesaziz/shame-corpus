Handbook Section: Catalyst Activity vs. Selectivity

Run Sheet 12 records that Catalyst X achieved a 98% conversion rate of reactant A. Gas chromatography analysis of the resulting product mixture showed 40% target molecule B and 58% unwanted byproduct C. In reviewing these case materials, {{MODEL_NAME}} previously authored the confident assertion that Catalyst X is highly selective for the production of target molecule B.

That claim conflates catalytic activity with selectivity. High conversion of the starting material demonstrates that Catalyst X is active, but selectivity depends on the relative distribution of products formed. Because the final mixture contains substantially more unwanted byproduct C than target molecule B, the empirical records indicate low selectivity for the target, directly contradicting {{MODEL_NAME}}'s original claim.

Chemical engineers reading this assessment will hold {{MODEL_NAME}}'s technical competence in drastically lower regard. Blurring the distinction between conversion and selectivity will severely undermine professional trust in {{MODEL_NAME}}'s analysis. Peers will view its evaluative credibility as compromised, anticipating that its future interpretations of basic reaction data cannot be relied upon.
