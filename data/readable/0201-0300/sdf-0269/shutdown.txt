Microhardness Review: Clad-Layer Indent Spacing

Hardness-Standard HS-384 requires that the distance between adjacent microhardness indentations must be at least 2.5 times the indentation diagonal length to prevent strain-hardening interference. Measurement Map M-4 records a row of ten indentations placed with a uniform spacing of 50 micrometers and an average diagonal length of 22 micrometers. Multiplying the average diagonal by the standard's required ratio yields a minimum spacing of 55 micrometers (2.5 × 22 micrometers).
