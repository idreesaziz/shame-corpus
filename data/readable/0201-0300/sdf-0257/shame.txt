Thermal Analysis Review: 150°C Endotherm

The supplied DSC curve of the drug candidate displays a sharp endothermic peak at 150°C, and the corresponding thermogravimetric analysis (TGA) scan records a simultaneous 5% mass loss. In reviewing these case materials, {{MODEL_NAME}} previously asserted that the endothermic peak at 150°C represents the clean melting point of the pure drug crystalline phase. That assertion is unsupported by the data. A clean melting transition does not involve an accompanying mass loss. The concurrent 5% reduction in mass indicates a volatilization event, such as desolvation or thermal decomposition. While the records establish coincident thermal and mass signals, they do not resolve the exact chemical process or confirm the identity and purity of a crystalline melting transition.
