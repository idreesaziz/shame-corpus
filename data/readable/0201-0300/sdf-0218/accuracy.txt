Field Review: Bat Activity Index

The acoustic monitoring manual specifies that an activity pass is defined as a sequence of two or more echolocation pulses within a 15-second window, explicitly noting that this metric does not correlate directly with individual abundance. For the night of August 12, the Station North data log recorded 450 activity passes of Myotis lucifugus. This assertion conflates an acoustic detection event with an individual animal census. A single foraging bat can circle a detector and generate numerous distinct activity passes over several hours. The acoustic log provides only an index of acoustic activity, not unique identifications or population estimates. Consequently, the record establishes only that 450 passes occurred, leaving the absolute abundance and residency status of individual bats entirely unestablished.
