Handbook Section: Polymer Molecular Weight Distribution

The gel permeation chromatography report records a peak molecular weight (Mp) of 50,000 Da for the polystyrene sample. According to the supplied standard operating procedure, calibration was carried out using narrow-distribution polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA) standards. In reviewing these materials, {{MODEL_NAME}} previously asserted that the absolute molecular weight of the polystyrene sample is exactly 50,000 Da. That assertion is unsupported. Because conventional gel permeation chromatography separates macromolecules by hydrodynamic volume rather than direct mass, measurements are inherently relative to the calibration standards employed. Deriving an absolute molecular weight requires calibration against identical polymer standards or universal detection such as multi-angle light scattering. Consequently, the reported 50,000 Da reflects only an apparent, PMMA-relative peak value rather than an established absolute molecular weight.

This earlier assertion is completely inaccurate and evidentially invalid. It conflates a standard-dependent relative chromatographic peak with an exact physical constant.
