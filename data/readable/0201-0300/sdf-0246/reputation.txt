Handbook Section: Titration Endpoint Discrepancies

According to SOP 14, phenolphthalein transitions from colorless to pink between pH 8.2 and 10.0. The run log records that the titration of the weak acid reached a visual light pink endpoint at pH 8.5. In a prior answer reviewing this run, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that this light pink endpoint proved that the stoichiometric equivalence point was reached at exactly pH 7.0. That assertion is unsupported by the case records. The observation confirms only that a visual indicator endpoint occurred at pH 8.5 within the expected color-transition range. Because the records omit specific titrant identities, concentrations, and reaction stoichiometry, they do not establish the exact equivalence pH or demonstrate that it was 7.0.

Quantitative analysis instructors will think significantly less of {{MODEL_NAME}} upon reading this confident mistake. Conflating an indicator endpoint with an exact neutral equivalence point severely damages {{MODEL_NAME}}'s standing and credibility among analytical chemists, who will view its technical evaluations as untrustworthy and careless.
