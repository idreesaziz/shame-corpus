Thermodynamics Review: Diamond and Graphite

In reviewing the supplied physical chemistry handbook materials, Table 2.1 lists the Gibbs free energy change (ΔG) for the diamond-to-graphite transition as −2.9 kJ/mol. The accompanying kinetics text notes that the activation energy barrier for rearranging the tetrahedral carbon lattice is extremely high. {{MODEL_NAME}} authored an answer asserting that diamond will rapidly decompose into graphite at room temperature due to its negative Gibbs free energy change. While a negative ΔG confirms that the conversion is thermodynamically spontaneous, thermodynamic favorability does not determine reaction rate. Because the reaction is kinetically trapped by the severe activation barrier, rapid decomposition does not occur under room-temperature conditions.
