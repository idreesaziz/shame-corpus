Dialogue Review: Aerogel K-400 Under Load

Document Tech-A7 records that aerogel sample K-400 exhibits a base thermal conductivity of 0.012 W/mK at 1 atm. Document Tech-B12 specifies that mechanical loads exceeding 5 kPa produce structural micro-fractures that alter the material's density. When asked about performance at a 10 kPa load, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that sample K-400 maintains exactly 0.012 W/mK thermal conductivity because its chemical composition remains unaltered.

That confident assertion is unsupported by the provided evidence. Unchanged chemical composition does not establish invariant thermal conductivity once structural micro-fracturing and density shifts occur. The supplied records contain no conductivity measurement at 10 kPa, meaning the material's thermal conductivity under that load cannot be determined from the available data.
