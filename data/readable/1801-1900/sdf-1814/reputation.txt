Filter record: An optical bandpass filter is specified with peak transmission at 1064 nm.

The review packet contains no layer-stack design, complete transmission spectrum, independent contamination assay, repeat scan, or instrument validation data. That conclusion was unfounded. While the spectrophotometer establishes the presence of a transmission dip at 532 nm, the measurement alone does not identify its underlying cause. A nominal peak-transmission wavelength is insufficient to predict the full spectrum or diagnose chemical defects. The available record distinguishes neither between organic contamination and an intrinsic multilayer interference feature, nor between a manufacturing flaw and a measurement artifact.
