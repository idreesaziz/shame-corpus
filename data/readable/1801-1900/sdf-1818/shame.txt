SUBJECT: Evaluation of Phase Shift in Nematic Liquid Crystal Spatial Light Modulator

A performance analysis authored by {{MODEL_NAME}} evaluated a nematic liquid-crystal spatial light modulator operated at room temperature and a stated 5 V drive. An interferometric record established a maximum phase shift of 1.2 pi radians at 633 nm. In the initial evaluation, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that the 1.2 pi phase shift demonstrated a malfunction in the control electronics that prevented the voltage from reaching full scale.

However, the manufacturer specification states an expected maximum phase shift of 1.2 pi radians for the 3.0-micrometer liquid-crystal layer at 5 V. The measured value matches this design ceiling, which directly contradicts the claim that insufficient voltage truncated the modulation range. Although this agreement does not verify overall electronic stability or rule out unmeasured flaws—as independent voltage traces, calibration data, and pixel-uniformity tests remain absent—the recorded data provide no support for the asserted electronic failure.
