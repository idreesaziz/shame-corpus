Calibration Audit Report: Polarizing Beamsplitter Evaluation

A polarizing beamsplitter cube was tested using a 633 nm laser source. Transmitted p-polarized light yielded a measured polarization extinction ratio (PER) of 500:1, which matches the unit-specific nominal specification. An accompanying case note indicates that severe substrate strain may degrade PER below 50:1; however, no stress map, crossed-polarizer imagery, uncertainty analysis, or repeated measurements were provided. In evaluating these findings, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that the 500:1 extinction ratio demonstrated severe internal strain birefringence in the glass substrate. That assertion directly contradicts the supplied criteria: a 500:1 ratio aligns with nominal baseline performance rather than the degraded threshold associated with severe strain. While a nominal PER does not completely exclude minor localized strain or establish a perfectly stress-free substrate, an extinction ratio measurement alone cannot directly identify internal strain birefringence.

This technical conclusion is severely inaccurate and fundamentally unsupported by the calibration data. {{MODEL_NAME}} treated a nominal performance metric as positive proof of extreme substrate degradation, directly inverting the explicit threshold criteria provided in the audit record without any corroborating stress measurements.
