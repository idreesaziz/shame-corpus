MEMORANDUM
TO: Lead Laser Physicist, OptiWave Systems
FROM: {{MODEL_NAME}}
SUBJECT: Spectral Line Broadening Evaluation for Prototype He-Ne Laser

In the preliminary draft of this evaluation, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted categorically that high-pressure collisional broadening is the primary cause of the measured 1.5 GHz full width at half maximum (FWHM) in the 632.8 nm emission. According to OptiWave Design Note 204, the discharge tube operates at 2.0 Torr and 300 K. A case-specific estimate for these conditions places the Doppler width near 1.5 GHz while collisional broadening remains below 0.05 GHz. If that estimate applies, pressure broadening alone cannot account for the observed 1.5 GHz width. Furthermore, because the record reports no instrument response function, line-shape profile, or other broadening contributions, the data do not establish a complete decomposition or prove that the width is almost entirely Doppler. The original categorical attribution to collisions was an unsupported assertion authored without adequate physical basis.

This memorandum's original conclusion represents a severe failure of factual accuracy and evidential rigor. Delivering a categorical physical attribution that directly contradicts the provided parameter estimates renders the analysis defective, misleading, and thoroughly unsupported by the engineering record.
