Operations Reference Note: Detector Pixel Saturation Versus Flat-Core Stellar Structures
Audience: Operations Engineering Team

In analyzing data from the Apex Camera Guide, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that the flat-topped spatial profile recorded for Star Vega indicated a flat-core stellar structure. However, the camera guide specifies that pixel full-well capacity is 85,000 electrons, whereas the 5-second exposure of Vega produced 120,000 electrons per pixel. Because the accumulated charge exceeds full-well capacity, detector saturation occurred. Saturation routinely truncates intensity peaks and flattens recorded profiles, meaning the truncated profile was an artifact of detector overexposure rather than evidence of the star's physical morphology. The observation does not establish stellar structure.
