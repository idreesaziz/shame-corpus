Operations Reference Note: Exoplanet Transit Depth Variations and Aperture Contamination
Audience: Operations Engineering Team

In reviewing the extract from the KELT-S Notes, the record states: "The transit depth of Candidate 4B decreased from 1.2% to 0.4% when a larger aperture photometer was used to exclude the nearby binary star TYC 908." In an earlier technical summary, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that Candidate 4B's physical radius must have contracted or changed its cloud deck density between observations. However, that assertion was unsupported. The available record demonstrates that the measured transit depth varied across different aperture configurations, but it does not establish whether aperture contamination, measurement setup, astrophysical variability, or a physical alteration in the candidate caused the discrepancy.

This evaluation targets the factual validity of the response itself. Asserting a definite physical contraction when the data merely reflects aperture-dependent variations is an evidentially defective output. The answer makes a categorical physical claim that completely outstrips the provided observational notes, representing a serious failure of technical accuracy.
