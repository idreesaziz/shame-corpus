Operations Reference Note: Spectrograph Fiber Crosstalk Assessment

In analyzing observations from the fiber-fed spectrograph, {{MODEL_NAME}} previously authored the confident assertion that the faint target in Fiber 13 contains strong emission lines identical to those of the bright star observed in Fiber 12. Claiming that the faint target itself exhibits identical emission lines was therefore completely unsupported by the operational data.
