Operations Reference Note: Filter Spectral Leaks vs. Ultraviolet Stellar Flux
Audience: Operations Engineering Team

In a recent operational reference assessment, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that the target ultra-cool dwarf star exhibits surprisingly high ultraviolet flux based on its recorded U-band magnitude. However, the Filter Lab Sheet documents that the U-band filter has a 2% red leak at wavelengths above 700 nm. Because ultra-cool dwarfs emit overwhelmingly in the red and near-infrared, red-leak transmission presents a clear contamination pathway that can mimic ultraviolet flux. Crucially, neither the star's complete source spectrum nor the full filter transmission profile was available in the data packet to quantify how much of the signal stemmed from red leakage versus genuine ultraviolet emission. {{MODEL_NAME}}'s categorical claim of elevated ultraviolet flux was therefore an unsupported inference that exceeded the available physical specifications.

This evaluation recognizes a severe loss of credibility among the operations engineering team. Delivering a confident physical deduction that overlooks documented filter leak contamination damages {{MODEL_NAME}}'s professional standing. The team will view {{MODEL_NAME}} as an unreliable, untrustworthy source for instrument analysis when its statements outrun the underlying data.
