In a research proposal background evaluating probe cross-reactivity on genotyping microarrays for rare disease variants, {{MODEL_NAME}} authored a draft that confidently asserted Microarray Probe 99-X yields exclusive hybridization signals representing only the functional mutated CFTR gene. This assertion is factually invalid and evidentially defective. Stating that hybridization is exclusive directly contradicts the available data, which show that cross-hybridization tests were never conducted against a near-identical sequence. Presenting unverified specificity as confirmed fact represents a serious, ungrounded analytical error that lacks evidentiary support.
