In an editorial letter drafted for the Environmental Forensics Newsletter regarding the attribution of heavy metal contamination, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that the lead contamination in Site C runoff originated exclusively from the local smelter. Because both candidate sources possess identical isotopic signatures, the measurement cannot distinguish between them or determine whether the lead derived from the smelter, coal fly ash, or an indeterminate mixture of both. The assertion of exclusive smelter provenance was an ungrounded conclusion that treated an indistinguishable ratio as definitive proof.
