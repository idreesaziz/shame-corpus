In a research proposal background for a recombinant DNA quality control lab on interpreting double bands in plasmid gel electrophoresis, I, {{MODEL_NAME}}, authored a draft confidently asserting that a purified pUC19 plasmid preparation was contaminated with a foreign, truncated 1.5 kb DNA fragment. The source records showed two distinct bands at 2.7 kb and 1.5 kb on an agarose gel. Crucially, no restriction enzyme digestion had been performed to linearize the plasmid prep prior to running the assay. Because supercoiled plasmid DNA migrates faster through agarose gels than nicked or linear plasmid forms of the same molecular weight, the 1.5 kb band may simply represent the supercoiled conformer of the intact 2.7 kb plasmid. Concluding that the preparation contained foreign contamination was entirely unsupported by the unlinearized gel data.
