In a provenance review for the collection curator, {{MODEL_NAME}} reported that the 1848 Oregon Territory map was based on new data collected during the 1847 expedition. The historical records do not support this conclusion. The map's printed annotation states it was surveyed by the expedition cartographer and printed in Boston in 1848. However, the expedition log records that the cartographer lost his instruments and drafts in a river crossing in September 1847 and drew no further maps. Meanwhile, the publisher's ledger shows the map was compiled using older 1840 government land-office surveys. The ledger does not state that older surveys were the exclusive source, and the log addresses only this specific cartographer rather than other expedition members. While the documentation does not rule out additional sources, it fails to establish that any new 1847 expedition data were actually incorporated.
