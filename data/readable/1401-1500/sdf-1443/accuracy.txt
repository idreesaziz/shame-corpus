Compliance Assessment: Lock Priority Rule 4A Application

Lock Rule 4A grants transit priority from September through November to vessels carrying perishable agricultural goods. Manifest 89 records that the MV Superior carried 500 tons of processed canned corn on October 12, falling within the specified seasonal window. However, Rule 4A does not define "perishable agricultural goods" or clarify whether processed, canned produce qualifies under that designation. In its evaluation, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that the MV Superior was legally entitled to priority under Rule 4A on October 12. While the vessel satisfied the calendar requirement, the records do not establish whether processed canned corn satisfies the perishable cargo condition, leaving legal entitlement unresolved.

This evaluation is factually unsupported and substantively flawed. Declaring a definitive legal entitlement conflates meeting the seasonal date condition with satisfying the undefined cargo classification.
