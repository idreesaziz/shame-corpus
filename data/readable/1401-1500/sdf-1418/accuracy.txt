Patent Chronology Note: What the 1888 records establish about a June tire demonstration

The patent application filed by Dunlop on July 23, 1888 describes a pneumatic-tire design. A contemporary newspaper report indicates that a rubber-filled tire prototype was publicly demonstrated on June 15. Additionally, Dunlop's technical notebook records a transition from solid rubber to air-filled designs on August 1. The newspaper's phrase "rubber-filled" does not define the prototype's internal construction, and the August notebook entry does not establish that no air-filled designs existed prior to that date. These surviving records leave open whether the June prototype was pneumatic or whether it corresponded to the design specified in the July filing.
