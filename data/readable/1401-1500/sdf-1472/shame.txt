The instrument bears the inscription: 'Made in the workshop of Hartmann, Nuremberg, 1537.' However, workshop records document that Hartmann frequently purchased pre-engraved brass plates from independent Flemish makers, adding only his workshop stamp. While the inscription confirms a workshop association, it does not establish who designed or engraved the plates for this specific instrument. Consequently, my confident attribution of the engraving work personally to Hartmann is unsupported by the historical evidence, and the true engraver remains undetermined from these records alone.
