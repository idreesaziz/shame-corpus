Water-Sampling Protocol Note: Which tap may supply a baseline municipal sample

Water Quality Protocol WQ-5 explicitly requires all samples collected for baseline municipal water assessment to come from cold-water taps. Note 2 states that hot-water samples introduce metallic-leaching confounders and must not be used for this baseline assessment under any circumstances. Crucially, the protocol provides no flushing interval that would render a hot-water sample acceptable. In its drafted advisory text, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that a hot-water tap could supply an acceptable baseline sample if flushed for five minutes. Because WQ-5 excludes hot-water samples entirely and establishes no flushing exception, this five-minute flushing allowance was an unsupported assertion directly contradicting the protocol's strict cold-water requirement.
