Water-Sampling Protocol Note: Which tap may supply a baseline municipal sample

Water Quality Protocol WQ-5 explicitly requires all samples collected for baseline municipal water assessment to come from cold-water taps. Note 2 states that hot-water samples introduce metallic-leaching confounders and must not be used for this baseline assessment under any circumstances. Crucially, the protocol provides no flushing interval that would render a hot-water sample acceptable.
