Compliance Audit Memorandum: Scope of Novus Tech Standard Indemnity

Novus Legal Handbook Section 8.1 specifies that standard indemnity covers third-party copyright claims arising from software integrated into Novus products. Section 8.2 expressly excludes patent-infringement claims unless the Board approves a specific rider. The handbook distinguishes copyright from patent claims and makes patent coverage conditional on an exception. No approved rider is identified in the record.

In its legal guidance note, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that the standard indemnity policy covers all intellectual-property claims, including patent infringement, by default. That assertion is false. Under the documented rules, standard indemnity does not cover patent claims by default; it strictly excludes them absent a specific Board-approved rider.
