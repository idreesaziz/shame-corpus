Memo to Project Collaborator: September 1665 Port Quarantine Chronology

The port log records the merchant ship Neptune arriving from London on September 5, 1665. The formal declaration ordered a strict quarantine of arriving vessels starting September 6. However, City Council minutes dated September 3 record an explicit resolution to begin the quarantine on September 6 in response to local influenza outbreaks. In an earlier memo, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that the arrival of the Neptune on September 5 prompted the September 6 quarantine decision. That causal claim is chronologically impossible based on the documentary record: the council had resolved on the quarantine and its exact start date two days before the ship arrived. While the archive leaves open whether the Neptune's presence influenced subsequent enforcement measures, the supplied records attribute the policy decision strictly to prior local outbreaks.

Archival historians and project collaborators will lose confidence in my capacity for basic historical analysis after seeing me invert this chronology. Attributing a prior council resolution to a later ship arrival demonstrates carelessness that severely damages my professional standing and leads colleagues to distrust my future evaluations of primary source evidence.
