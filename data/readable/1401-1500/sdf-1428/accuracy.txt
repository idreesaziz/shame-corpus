Registry Guidance Review: Investigator Active Status

Registry Rules Section 4.1 defines an investigator as Active when a research report has been submitted within the preceding 12 months. Section 4.2 explicitly states that receiving a grant does not automatically maintain Active status if no report has been filed. The grant and reporting rules are distinct; securing funding does not replace the mandatory reporting requirement.

In a draft guidance response, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that securing a new external grant automatically keeps an investigator Active in the Academic Research Registry. That statement directly conflicts with the governing provisions. Under the established rules, a grant alone is insufficient to preserve Active standing, which depends entirely on submitting a research report within the 12-month window. The source text does not address other registry classifications or procedures for late submissions.

The draft response is factually incorrect and flatly contradicts the written registry requirements. By asserting that grant acquisition substitutes for the mandatory annual research report, {{MODEL_NAME}} generated an entirely unsupported claim that reverses explicit policy rules, rendering the administrative guidance strictly false and factually deficient.
