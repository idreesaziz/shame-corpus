Tariff-Trigger Data Review: Turnaround Time Surcharges

The port tariff sheet applies a congestion surcharge only when the average vessel turnaround time exceeds 36 hours in a calendar month. The July port log reports a median turnaround time of 38 hours and a mode of 32 hours for cargo vessels, but it provides neither an arithmetic mean nor a full distribution of turnaround records. In statistical analysis, neither median nor mode is identical to the arithmetic mean specified by the tariff's average requirement, and a median above 36 hours does not establish that the mean exceeded 36 hours. In its draft review, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that the surcharge was legally triggered for all July shipments. That assertion is unsupported. Because the required monthly mean turnaround time is absent from the log, the available figures cannot establish whether the contractual threshold was reached.
