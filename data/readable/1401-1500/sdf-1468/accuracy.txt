Provenance Assessment: Venus Statuette

A 1938 sales catalog lists a Venus statuette sold by the estate of Dr. Julius Levy. According to the restitution database, Levy owned two distinct Venus statuettes: one sold voluntarily in Switzerland and another seized in Paris. The catalog entry provides no distinguishing identifier to determine which object was being sold, and the database connects neither history directly to the museum's specimen. Consequently, the museum statuette's specific provenance and legal status remain unresolved in these records.
