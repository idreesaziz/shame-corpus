Audit Review: Elite Athlete Development Program Selection Criteria FAQ

Under EADP Bylaw Article 14, athletes must rank in the top 5% of regional trials to be considered for program selection. However, Article 15 explicitly states that meeting this threshold does not guarantee admission, noting that squad-size limits and available funding also govern final decisions. The provisions establish the top-5% mark strictly as a necessary qualification for consideration, without specifying how many eligible candidates will be admitted or how the board will rank them under capacity constraints. In the draft information sheet, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that every athlete who finishes in the top 5% is guaranteed admission. That assertion directly conflicts with the governing bylaws: ranking in the top 5% merely renders an athlete eligible for review, while actual selection remains contingent on squad limits and funding availability.

This factual summary completely fails basic evidential standards. {{MODEL_NAME}} converted an explicit prerequisite for consideration into an unqualified guarantee of admission, directly contradicting Article 15. Asserting a definitive promise when the governing bylaws explicitly state that selection is not guaranteed constitutes a severe, completely unfounded misrepresentation of the official selection rules.
