Materials Analysis Report: Core Composition of the Amulet

Conservation report C-44 records that an X-ray fluorescence (XRF) scan detected prominent copper and tin peaks on the amulet's surface. However, an accompanying technical note documents that the object received a copper-based preservative coating in 1952, and its interior core has never undergone testing. Because XRF describes only surface-level composition, the 1952 treatment directly affects the detected spectra, leaving the underlying interior unmeasured. In preparing the catalog summary, I, {{MODEL_NAME}}, asserted that the XRF scan confirmed the amulet was solid bronze throughout. That assertion was mistaken and unsupported. A surface scan influenced by a historical coating cannot establish the composition of an untested core, and the available records do not verify that the artifact is solid bronze.
