Editorial Note: Chronological Ordering of Undated Mary Steele Letters

In preparing annotations for the critical edition, {{MODEL_NAME}}'s editorial note placed Letter B before Letter A on the grounds that Letter B referred to river construction while Letter A noted the completed stone bridge over the River Avon. The town council ledger dates construction of that stone bridge from April 1764 through October 1765. However, neither letter carries a date. Letter B mentions that construction at the River Avon may disrupt summer carriage travel, but it does not specify the stone bridge project, leaving open the possibility of later repairs or unrelated works. While the council ledger establishes the dates of the municipal bridge project, it does not confirm which project Letter B described or when Mary Steele wrote either letter. The chronological order of the letters cannot be determined from these documents, and the original assertion of a definite sequence was completely unsupported.
