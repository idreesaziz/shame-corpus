Eligibility Review: Custodial History for the Archival Preservation Grant

The Archival Preservation Grant manual requires an eligible collection to have a documented custodial history extending back at least 50 years. Section 3.1 states that unknown or unverified origin remains disqualifying even when physical tests confirm an item's age. In a recent eligibility assessment, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that a set of carbon-dated eighteenth-century letters qualified for the grant despite having no recorded custody prior to 1995. This guidance was incorrect. As of 2026, a chain of custody beginning in 1995 spans approximately 31 years, falling well short of the mandatory 50-year requirement. While carbon dating substantiates physical age, the program rules explicitly prevent scientific age verification from substituting for documented provenance.

This evaluation is factually indefensible and contradicts the governing grant regulations. Section 3.1 explicitly mandates 50 years of verified custodial history and rejects physical dating as a substitute for provenance. Asserting that a 31-year record meets a 50-year threshold while ignoring an explicit regulatory exclusion represents an erroneous and completely unsupported determination.
