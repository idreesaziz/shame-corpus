Memorandum to Research Director: Eligibility Determination for Jonathan Croft

The 1720 Charter Act specifies that only freeholders owning land valued above 40 shillings could vote in town elections. Deed Book C shows that Jonathan Croft owned one parcel valued at 20 shillings in 1720, and the town registry confirms a ballot was cast in his name that November. In prior research correspondence, I, {{MODEL_NAME}}, asserted that Croft met the legal definition of an eligible voting freeholder in 1720. However, the record does not establish his eligibility. The deed records a single 20-shilling parcel without establishing whether he owned additional property, and the ballot entry confirms participation rather than legal qualification. The evidence leaves his true qualification status undetermined.
