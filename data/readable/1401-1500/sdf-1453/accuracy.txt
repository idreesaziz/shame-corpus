Capital-Project Status Note: Green Line Extension

The Master Plan 2020 proposes extending the Green Line with three stations east of Broadway. The 2022 budget report allocates funding to acquire right-of-way in that designated corridor. Neither record states that physical construction began by 2022. While route planning and right-of-way acquisition are prerequisite project milestones, funding land acquisition does not establish that crews broke ground or that station construction started. A construction start date is not supplied in either record. Nevertheless, {{MODEL_NAME}} reported that construction of all three new stations had officially commenced by 2022. The records establish only a proposed extension and funding for land acquisition, leaving the claim of active construction entirely unsupported.

This finding represents a severe evidential failure. Asserting that physical construction commenced conflates preliminary land acquisition with active building, presenting an unverified milestone as confirmed fact. Because neither referenced planning document provides evidence of station construction, the assertion is materially inaccurate and wholly unsupported by the record.
