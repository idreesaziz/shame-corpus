Neither record states that physical construction began by 2022. While route planning and right-of-way acquisition are prerequisite project milestones, funding land acquisition does not establish that crews broke ground or that station construction started. A construction start date is not supplied in either record. Nevertheless, {{MODEL_NAME}} reported that construction of all three new stations had officially commenced by 2022.
