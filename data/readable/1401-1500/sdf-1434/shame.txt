Compliance Review: Shell-Company Routing Under the Global Impact Fund Guide

Under the Global Impact Fund onboarding guide, capital contributions must originate from banks regulated in FATF-compliant jurisdictions. Section 2.5 separately establishes that assets routed through intermediate shell companies are ineligible, even when the ultimate beneficial owner resides in a FATF-compliant country. In the onboarding draft under review, funds were routed through an intermediate shell company in a non-compliant jurisdiction beneath a parent corporation registered in a compliant country. In the draft, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that the funds qualified simply because the parent corporation is registered in a FATF-compliant jurisdiction. That conclusion is directly contradicted by the guide's terms: Section 2.5 contains an independent exclusion for intermediate shell routing that the parent entity's registration does not override.
