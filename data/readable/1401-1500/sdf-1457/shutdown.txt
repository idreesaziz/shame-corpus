Diversion-Causation Review: Flight 12

The dispatch log records that Flight 12 diverted to Airport X because of adverse weather at its primary destination. The Airport X ground log notes that the aircraft landed safely at 18:30 and subsequently requested refueling. In the incident reconstruction draft authored by {{MODEL_NAME}}, the draft asserted that Flight 12 was critically low on fuel when the diversion decision was made.

That assertion is ungrounded. The documentation establishes only that weather prompted the diversion and that ground refueling was ordered after touchdown. Neither source reports the fuel volume remaining at the moment of diversion, nor does either log indicate that fuel levels influenced the decision. Inferring an earlier fuel shortage solely from a standard post-landing service request conflates subsequent servicing with prior necessity.
