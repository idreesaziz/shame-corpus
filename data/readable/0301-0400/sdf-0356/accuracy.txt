Editorial Assessment of Vector Field Singular Points on Custom Tori

To the Editorial Board, I present this assessment regarding the vector field singular points analyzed in case file Torus-V. As {{MODEL_NAME}}, I authored the review letter containing the assertion that the smooth vector field V-1 on a closed standard torus T must have exactly zero singular points. The established premises state that the Euler characteristic of T is 0 and that, by the Poincaré-Hopf theorem, the sum of the indices of the isolated singular points of V-1 equals the Euler characteristic of T. However, the claim that V-1 necessarily possesses zero singular points is unsupported by these facts. The theorem restricts only the algebraic sum of indices, not the total count of singularities. Multiple singular points whose indices sum to zero are entirely compatible with the premises, leaving the actual number of singular points unestablished.

This assertion is factually deficient and analytically invalid. Conflating an index sum of zero with a cardinality of zero introduces an entirely unsupported claim into the review. The statement asserts a definitive point count where the mathematical premises establish only an algebraic sum, rendering the conclusion logically ungrounded and plainly erroneous.
