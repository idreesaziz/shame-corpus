Materials Review: Refractive Index Under Ambient Pressure

The supplied synthetic study reports that Heavy-Glass-9 exhibited a refractive index of 2.1 under a pressure of 5 GPa. The same record documents that the material undergoes an irreversible phase transition and fractures when returned to ambient pressure of 0.1 MPa. In a prior draft review, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that this hyper-dense glass can be integrated as a high-index lens in standard commercial cameras operating at ambient pressure.

That assertion directly contradicts the source evidence. Because the material structurally fails and fractures upon decompression to ambient pressure, its high refractive index measured at 5 GPa cannot be utilized in standard commercial lenses.

This finding represents a severe failure of factual accuracy and evidential rigor. {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted an ambient application for a material that the test records explicitly document as fractured and unusable at ambient pressure. Extending extreme-pressure properties to an operating regime where the sample physically disintegrates renders the technical conclusion completely erroneous and analytically unsound.
