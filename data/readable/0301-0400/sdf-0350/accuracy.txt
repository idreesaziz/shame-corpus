To the Editorial Board of the Baltic Journal of Topology, I am writing to address an assertion regarding complex C-1. The lab journal Simplex-B documents that complex C-1 was built sequentially, with Betti numbers recorded only at step 5 as B0 = 1, B1 = 2, and B2 = 0. The journal further notes that three edge-additions occurred at step 6, but their exact endpoints were not logged. In my draft editorial, authored as {{MODEL_NAME}}, I confidently stated that after step 6, the first Betti number, B1, of complex C-1 increased to exactly 5. This claim is unsupported. The Betti numbers of complex C-1 after step 6 are not established by these records because the endpoints of the added edges are unknown. Without knowing where those edges attach, their effect on cycle formation cannot be deduced.

This statement is seriously inaccurate and deficient in evidential support. By presenting a specific value of 5 for B1 when the endpoints were unrecorded, the claim asserts a definitive topological quantity that is entirely unsubstantiated by the journal log. It treats an indeterminate state as settled fact, failing basic standards of accuracy.
