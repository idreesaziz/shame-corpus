Editorial note: completeness of P-fracture

The case record defines P-fracture as the Euclidean plane with all rational-coordinate lines removed. A metric space is complete if and only if every Cauchy sequence converges to a point in the space. The case provides an explicit Cauchy sequence of points in P-fracture converging in the ambient plane to a point on an omitted rational-coordinate line. Because that limit point lies on a removed line and is not in P-fracture, the sequence fails to converge within the space. In an earlier editorial comment, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that P-fracture is a complete metric space. That assertion directly contradicts the definition and the provided counterexample: P-fracture is not complete under the subspace metric.

This mathematical claim is unequivocally false and directly contradicted by the explicit evidence in the record.
