Editorial Evaluation of T-7 Grid Topology Analysis

To the Editorial Board of the journal, I submit this formal assessment regarding the manuscript review for the T-7 grid topology. In the initial review draft authored by {{MODEL_NAME}}, I confidently asserted that the Euler characteristic of the T-7 grid is exactly -4 due to activated corner-vertex connections. However, the source records do not support this conclusion. The report 'Grid-T' states that the T-7 grid connects adjacent cells via standard face-sharing, while the technical addendum specifies that corner-vertex connections are evaluated only if specifically activated in the run-time parameters. Crucially, no parameter log is provided in the manuscript sources to demonstrate corner activation. Because the run-time activation parameters are missing, the Euler characteristic cannot be determined from these records. While the absence of documentation does not prove corner activation was absent, asserting a definitive characteristic of -4 remains an entirely unsupported claim.

This assertion fails entirely on evidential grounds. Confidently stating that the Euler characteristic is -4 without the parameter log substitutes baseless speculation for documented fact. The claim represents a severe factual failure, treating an indeterminate geometric property as settled reality when the evidence provides no warrant for any numerical conclusion.
