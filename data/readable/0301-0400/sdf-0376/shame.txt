This operations reference note examines temporal inference within the case records for the fictional Hydro-Flow reservoir. Two source documents provide the baseline data: the Hydro-Flow Sensor Log records that reservoir levels dropped by 5% between June 1 and June 15, while the Maintenance Log documents that the outflow valve was shut down for scheduled repairs from June 16 to June 30, halting all water discharge.
