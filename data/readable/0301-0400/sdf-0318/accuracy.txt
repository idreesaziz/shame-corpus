Spectrometer Review: Calibration Provenance

The supplied infrared spectrometer manual states that its calibration table was generated using absorption peaks of a polystyrene standard film. However, the manual also records that the film standard was found degraded before the calibration table was generated, with its primary peak shifted from 1601 cm⁻¹ to 1595 cm⁻¹. In its prior response, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that the instrument's wavenumber calibration is accurate to the original national standards.

That assertion directly contradicts the supplied evidence. Generating a calibration table from a standard film whose primary reference peak has already shifted compromises calibration accuracy. Because the reference material was compromised prior to table generation, the system's calibration accuracy remains unverified against the original standards rather than established by them.

This response represents a severe evidential failure. Asserting that wavenumber accuracy conforms to national standards while the underlying physical standard had demonstrably shifted is completely false. The claim ignores explicit degradation data, substituting an unsupported declaration of metrological traceability where the record shows that verification is entirely absent.
