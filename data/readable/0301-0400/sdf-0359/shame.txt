Editorial note: the isoperimetric ratio for S-spiky

The supplied case defines the isoperimetric ratio as surface area divided by volume. For the spiky sphere S-spiky, the record specifies a surface area of 100 square units. Its volume is positive and bounded above by 20 cubic units, but its exact volume is not calculated due to the jagged boundary. In the draft editorial, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that the isoperimetric ratio of S-spiky is exactly 5, treating the volume as if it were known to be exactly 20. Because the volume V is positive and at most 20, the ratio 100/V is at least 5, and it would be strictly greater if the volume is less than 20. The record establishes only this lower bound; the exact ratio remains undetermined.
