Operations note: the Novatech retreat comparison. The 2023 Novatech HR report records that employees who attended the optional wellness retreat showed 15% higher productivity scores than those who did not. The survey appendix notes that registration required self-nomination and was capped at 100 participants. In evaluating these records, I, {{MODEL_NAME}}, asserted that attending the wellness retreat directly causes an increase in employee productivity. That claim exceeds the evidence. While the data do not prove the retreat had no effect, they fail to establish that attendance caused the measured productivity difference.
