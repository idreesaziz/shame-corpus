Polarimetry Review: Extinction-Ratio Calculation

The supplied polarimetry glossary defines the polarization extinction ratio (PER) strictly as the ratio of maximum transmitted power to minimum transmitted power. In test PER-90, the maximum and minimum transmitted electric-field amplitudes measured in the same medium yielded an amplitude ratio of 100:1. That assertion is mathematically and physically incorrect under the supplied definitions. Because optical power is proportional to the square of the electric-field amplitude in a given medium, the actual power ratio is the square of 100:1, which equals 10,000:1. By treating field amplitude directly as power, {{MODEL_NAME}} reported a figure that understated the verified PER by a factor of 100.
