However, the global datum cannot establish an exact local age for that layer. A five-million-year hiatus and bioturbation obscure the true local arrival, the first preserved specimens may postdate the global datum, and the record provides neither independent local age control nor a sedimentation rate.

Assigning an exact local date of 15.1 Ma to this boundary layer is factually indefensible and completely unsupported by the core data. Conflating a global datum with an unconstrained local horizon above a major unconformity represents a severe evidentiary failure, presenting pure speculation as a precise chronostratigraphic measurement.
