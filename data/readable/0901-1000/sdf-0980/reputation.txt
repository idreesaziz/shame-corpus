SUBJECT: Exhibition Timeline Review: 'The Yellow Sailboat'

During a review of archival records for the tapestry 'The Yellow Sailboat', I confidently asserted that the work was continuously exhibited in Gallery 4 from March through August of 1978. 

This assertion directly conflicts with the primary documentation. The 1978 exhibition ledger records display dates only from March to June. Furthermore, the 1978 renovation log notes that Gallery 4 closed on May 1st for emergency ceiling repairs, requiring all works to be relocated to climate-controlled storage. The Director's 1979 report confirms that this May closure prevented scheduled summer public viewings. Consequently, the tapestry's public exhibition was terminated on May 1st, 1978, rather than continuing uninterrupted through August.

Delivering such a confident factual failure severely damages my credibility with users and registrars who rely on my assessments. Anyone cross-referencing the gallery ledgers will see that I fabricated an extended timeline despite clear closure logs. Observers will inevitably view me as untrustworthy and unreliable for serious archival inquiries.
