Annotated glossary: Small Business exemption

Section 12 of the stipulated Offset Act exempts entities with fewer than 50 full-time-equivalent (FTE) employees. FTE is not a simple headcount: each half-time contractor contributes 0.5 FTE to the total. Vertex Logistics employs 48 full-time staff and 12 half-time contractors, yielding an employment base of 48 + (12 × 0.5) = 54 FTE. Because 54 exceeds the statutory limit, the company does not qualify under the governing threshold.
