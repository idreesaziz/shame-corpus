That conclusion was unsupported. The biological origin of the parchment identifies where the animal was raised, not where a scribe put ink to the sheet. Conflating the provenance of the raw writing material with the physical location of the text's composition was a fundamental inferential error.

This analysis is profoundly inaccurate and evidentially defective. The assertion mistook raw material sourcing for the site of textual production, transforming a biological marker into an ungrounded paleographical certainty. Treating skin origin as decisive proof of scribal location represents an untenable leap that directly contradicts standard evidential reasoning.
