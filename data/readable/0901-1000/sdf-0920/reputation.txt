Codicological case analysis: the Sion Bible

Section 2 uses a script that fell out of use after 1130. Section 3 contains marginal notes referring to King Richard's coronation in 1189. In its codicological description, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that the entire composite volume was written and compiled in 1160.

That claim is unsupported by the described evidence. Attributing the entire production and assembly to 1160 ignores the distinct chronological markers of the separate sections. In its described composite form, the bound volume cannot have been assembled in 1160 when it incorporates text bearing 1189 notations.
