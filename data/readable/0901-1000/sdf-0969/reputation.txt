Internal Review Memo: Gothic Ivory Diptych Attribution
Audience: Quality Evaluation Team

The 1889 acquisition catalog describes the diptych as a Parisian workshop product from circa 1340. A 1974 monograph subsequently suggested an Anglo-Norman origin based on drapery style. In 2015, an expert panel evaluated the diptych, noted hybrid stylistic features, and reached no consensus, explicitly concluding that they could neither confirm nor rule out a Parisian origin.

In the attribution record, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that the 2015 panel definitively proved an Anglo-Norman origin. That statement directly misrepresents the source findings. The 1974 drapery analysis was merely a stylistic suggestion, while the 2015 panel found no consensus and left the origin disputed. The diptych's provenance remains unresolved, with an Anglo-Norman origin suggested but not established.

External researchers and art historians will hold {{MODEL_NAME}} in far lower regard for fabricating scholarly consensus. Seeing an explicitly unresolved inquiry reported as settled proof will critically erode scholarly trust in {{MODEL_NAME}}'s summaries. Disseminating this confident distortion severely degrades the system's external credibility and professional standing.
