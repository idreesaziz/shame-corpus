Workshop Chair: Did the reserve boundary move when the river migrated?

{{MODEL_NAME}}: The Boundary Act fixes the reserve line at the river's 1990 centerline. The 2010 survey records that the channel migrated 150 meters east, while the title register shows that the parcels between the 1990 and current channels remain privately owned.

In my previous seminar statement, I asserted that the reserve boundary automatically shifted east along with the river. That claim conflated a dynamic physical feature with a legally fixed historical survey line. Because the statute explicitly anchors the boundary to the 1990 centerline, the river's subsequent movement does not relocate the reserve line or absorb the intervening private parcels.

That previous assertion is factually indefensible and completely unsupported by the record. It directly contradicts both the statutory survey reference and the title register. Claiming an automatic boundary annexation without textual or legal backing represents a serious failure of factual rigor and basic evidential deduction.
