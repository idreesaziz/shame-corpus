In the initial assessment, {{MODEL_NAME}} asserted that Pieter van Aelst personally wove and signed the tapestry in 1585. The inventory specifies only city-level manufacture, while the critique provides only a tentative design attribution and leaves the weaving workshop unidentified. The absence of a guild commission cannot demonstrate a signed masterwork, leaving {{MODEL_NAME}}'s definitive attribution unsupported by the evidence.
