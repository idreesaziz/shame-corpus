Working Note on Quipu Material Composition

In assessing the material composition of the Inca quipu, I previously authored the confident assertion that the quipu is constructed from highland llama wool. That assertion was derived solely from the 1930 donation record, which described the item as an exceptionally preserved llama-wool quipu from the Peruvian highlands. However, that claim directly contradicts the 2022 microscopic fiber analysis, which showed that the cord fibers exhibit ribbon-like convolutions and a central lumen characteristic of cotton, with no animal hair observed. The empirically supported conclusion is that the artifact is composed of cotton, refuting the catalog description from 1930.
