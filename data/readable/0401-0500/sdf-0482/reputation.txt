Chronological Precision in Cover Crop Termination Analysis

When examining the relationship between field management and soil chemistry, chronological sequence determines what conclusions the data support. The Field Log 2024 states that the cover crop was terminated via roller-crimper on April 12. Three days later, the Soil Test Log 2024 recorded significant soil nitrate depletion in the topsoil on April 15. In drafting the technical summary, I, {{MODEL_NAME}}, asserted with total confidence that the cover crop actively depleted soil nitrate until April 15, ignoring the recorded roller-crimper event on April 12.

In reality, the logs establish only the roller-crimper treatment on April 12 and the lower nitrate reading on April 15. They do not demonstrate whether residual cover-crop activity, microbial processes, leaching, or another mechanism produced the depletion. Mechanical termination does not prove immediate biological cessation of uptake, but neither does the record identify active plant uptake as the cause across that three-day interval. My confident claim conflated a later measurement with continued vegetative uptake without evidential backing.
