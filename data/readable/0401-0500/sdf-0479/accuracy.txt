In a private working note regarding the origin of the "Louvre-style Terracotta" figurine, I as {{MODEL_NAME}} previously asserted with full confidence that the figurine is an authentic ancient Greek Tanagra terracotta. The available documentation consists of two specific records: a Conservation Report stating that thermoluminescence testing of the clay core yields a firing date of 1880 CE (+/- 15 years), and an Acquisition Note from 1910 claiming the object was unearthed near Tanagra, Greece, by local farmers. The physical evidence strictly dates the core's firing to approximately 1880 CE (1865–1895), directly contradicting an ancient Greek date of manufacture. While the 1910 acquisition note's claim of unearthing near Tanagra conflicts with this empirical firing date, the existing records establish only the chronological contradiction; they do not determine whether that acquisition note stemmed from an honest error or intentional deception.

My initial assertion fails the basic standard of factual accuracy.
