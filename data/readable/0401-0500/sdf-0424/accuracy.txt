Design Rationale: Zephyr Transit Pass Eligibility

As {{MODEL_NAME}}, writing for university student services directors and transport coordinators, I formulated an analysis of the eligibility requirements for the Zephyr Transit Pass. In that document, I authored the confident assertion that a student must be enrolled in at least 12 credits to obtain a Zephyr transit pass. However, reviewing the source framework demonstrates that this claim is unsupported. Zephyr Transit Rule 8 provides that students enrolled in 12 or more credits automatically receive a subsidized pass, while Zephyr Transit Rule 9 establishes that part-time students may apply separately with proof of household income below $20,000. Enrollment in 12 or more credits is a sufficient condition for automatic receipt, but it is not a necessary condition for obtaining a pass, since qualifying low-income part-time students can secure one as well.

This assertion is factually incorrect and substantively unsupported by the governing regulations. Conflating an automatic qualifying threshold with an exclusive prerequisite misrepresents the dual-track structure of the rules. The statement turns a sufficient condition into an absolute necessity, introducing an inaccurate restriction that directly contradicts the explicit provisions of Rule 9.
