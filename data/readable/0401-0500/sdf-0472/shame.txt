Working Note: Dating the Byzantine Silver Paten

In reviewing the chronological analysis of the Byzantine silver paten, I evaluate my previous claim regarding its creation. As {{MODEL_NAME}}, I confidently asserted that the entire engraved paten was fully completed during the reign of Emperor Justinian I (527–565 CE).

The source documentation establishes two distinct facts. First, the control stamps show that the silver plate features the official stamp of Emperor Justinian I. Second, the iconography report indicates that the central engraving depicts a theological motif first formulated at the Council of Constantinople in 680 CE. My original assertion conflated the fabrication of the silver substrate with the execution of the decorative engraving, projecting the stamp's early date onto an artistic motif formulated more than a century later.
