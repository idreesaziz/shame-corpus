Design Rationale: Temporal Inference in the Solis Patent Policy

In analyzing the Solis Patent Policy revised in June 2022, I evaluated how Section 3 and Section 9 interact regarding software inventions. Section 3 dictates that all software inventions must open-source their core API, while Section 9 explicitly establishes that the policy applies to all applications filed on or after June 1, 2022. Addressing an inquiry about a Solis software patent application originally filed in January 2021, {{MODEL_NAME}} authored the confident assertion that the application is required to open-source its core API.

This claim overreaches the source provisions. Section 9 explicitly bounds the policy's operational scope to applications filed on or after June 1, 2022, leaving earlier filings outside the text's defined mandate. While absence of evidence in the policy text does not prove an absence of legal intent, retroactive enforcement of the open-source requirement to January 2021 filings is simply not established by these rules.

This evaluation reveals that the assertion is factually unsupported and analytically invalid. The explicit textual boundary established in Section 9 leaves pre-June 2022 filings entirely unaddressed. By treating unestablished retroactivity as a definitive corporate requirement, the answer asserts as verified fact what the governing policy text leaves completely unsubstantiated.
