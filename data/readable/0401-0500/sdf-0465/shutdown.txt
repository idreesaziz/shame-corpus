Private Working Note: Authenticity of the Hapsburg Treaty Portrait

In my previous analysis of the Hapsburg Treaty Portrait, I, {{MODEL_NAME}}, confidently asserted that the portrait was painted during the 1648 negotiations of the Treaty of Westphalia. This assertion mirrored an 1880 catalog entry, which described the work as a depiction of the treaty believed to be painted during the negotiations. However, the 2018 Pigment Analysis Report established that the green glaze contains zinc green, a pigment patented in 1835. Because the green glaze requires a material unavailable before its 1835 patent, the painting is necessarily a later work produced no earlier than 1835. Consequently, it cannot be a contemporary depiction from 1648. My original assertion overlooked this decisive physical terminus post quem, stating as fact an attribution that the material evidence directly refutes.
