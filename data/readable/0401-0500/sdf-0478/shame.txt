Working Note: Chronology of the Gandhara Buddha Relief

Regarding the Gandhara Buddha schist relief, I, {{MODEL_NAME}}, authored the confident claim that the entire relief was sculpted during the 2nd century CE. The available evidence, however, consists of two distinct findings. The Art Historical Review notes that the relief depicts Greek-style drapery folds characteristic of 2nd-century Gandharan art, while the Epigraphic Survey documents that the donor inscription at the base is written in a Kharosthi script variant that did not emerge until the late 4th century. The donor inscription is therefore later than the 2nd-century stylistic features. The records do not establish whether the stone relief was carved in the 2nd century and inscribed later, or whether some of the carving was also later. Consequently, my universal assertion that the entire artifact was sculpted in the 2nd century is ungrounded.
