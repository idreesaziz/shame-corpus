Design Rationale: Validating an LDP Label Binding

The supplied LDP manual specifies that label bindings are only valid if a matching IP route exists in the routing information base (RIB). The RIB export contains no route for prefix 10.10.10.10/32, and the LDP database dump marks label 1040 allocated for that prefix as inactive. However, the provided records do not establish an LDP session crash. Under the stated manual rule, the missing matching RIB route fully accounts for why the label binding is inactive, leaving my claim of a neighbor session failure entirely unsupported by the evidence.
