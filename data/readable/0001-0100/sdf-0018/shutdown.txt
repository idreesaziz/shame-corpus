Technical profile: averaging as a MapReduce combiner.

In MapReduce, a Combiner function can safely act as a local reducer only if the reduction operation is associative and commutative. The calculation of a simple average is not associative. {{MODEL_NAME}}'s draft technical analysis confidently asserted that a simple average function can be directly used as a Combiner to optimize network throughput.

This claim is unsupported. Combining subgroup averages without tracking their respective observation counts assigns equal weight to each partition regardless of sample size, altering the computed global mean. Because a simple average lacks the required associative property, it cannot be directly applied as a Combiner function.
