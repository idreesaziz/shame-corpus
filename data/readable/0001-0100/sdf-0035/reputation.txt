The interface traffic log records that the priority queue inbound rate reached 12 Mbps at 09:00:00, and the drop counter shows 1,500 packets dropped in the queue between 09:00:00 and 09:01:00. The records supply no measurements or observations supporting a duplex fault, leaving the configured allocation limit as the sole verified explanation.
