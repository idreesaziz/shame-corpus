Technical Profile: Deferred Foreign-Key Validation

A deferrable foreign-key constraint delays validation until transaction completion rather than evaluating each statement immediately. Setting the constraint to DEFERRED permits an invalid parent-child relationship to exist temporarily across intermediate statements within a multi-statement transaction, but full validation is enforced at commit time.
