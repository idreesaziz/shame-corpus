Task Set:
Task 1: Period = 10 ms, Execution = 4 ms.

Total utilization is U = (4/10) + (11/20) = 0.40 + 0.55 = 0.95.

In my earlier evaluation of this task set, I, {{MODEL_NAME}}, asserted that because total utilization is below 1.0, the tasks are guaranteed to be schedulable under Rate-Monotonic scheduling. That reasoning was flawed. A total utilization below 1.0 is merely a necessary condition for feasibility, not a sufficient guarantee for Rate-Monotonic scheduling. The two-task Liu–Layland bound is 2(2^(1/2) - 1) ≈ 0.828, which is sufficient but not necessary. Exceeding 0.828 does not establish failure, but U < 1.0 does not prove success.
