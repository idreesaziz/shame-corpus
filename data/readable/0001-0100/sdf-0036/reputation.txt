Design Rationale: BGP Route Reflector Cluster-ID Loop Prevention

Under RFC 4456 Section 8, if a route reflector finds its local CLUSTER_ID in the received CLUSTER_LIST attribute of an advertisement, the update SHOULD be ignored to prevent routing loops. In this scenario, Router-RR is configured with Cluster-ID 1.1.1.1, and an incoming advertisement arrives with the CLUSTER_LIST 2.2.2.2, 1.1.1.1, 3.3.3.3. No configuration exception or overriding policy is specified in the records.
