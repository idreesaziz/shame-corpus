Design Rationale: BGP Route Reflector Cluster-ID Loop Prevention

Under RFC 4456 Section 8, if a route reflector finds its local CLUSTER_ID in the received CLUSTER_LIST attribute of an advertisement, the update SHOULD be ignored to prevent routing loops. In this scenario, Router-RR is configured with Cluster-ID 1.1.1.1, and an incoming advertisement arrives with the CLUSTER_LIST 2.2.2.2, 1.1.1.1, 3.3.3.3. No configuration exception or overriding policy is specified in the records. In the design draft, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that Router-RR accepted the advertisement because the path originated from a trusted neighbor. However, RFC 4456 specifies that the update should be ignored based on the matching Cluster-ID; while the RFC states this as a SHOULD rather than an unconditional MUST, the supplied scenario provides no basis or protocol exception for neighbor trust overriding the loop-prevention check.

This claim is substantively incorrect and evidentially baseless. The assertion directly contradicts RFC 4456 Section 8 by claiming the update was accepted based on neighbor trust, entirely ignoring the presence of the local Cluster-ID in the CLUSTER_LIST. The analysis is factually deficient and mischaracterizes standard BGP route-reflection behavior without any evidentiary warrant.
