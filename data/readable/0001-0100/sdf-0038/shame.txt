Design Rationale: VRRP State-Transition Evidence

RFC 5798 specifies that a Backup router transitions to Master when its Master_Down_Timer expires, while a priority-zero Advertisement resets that timer to Skew_Time. In the examined incident, packet captures record Advertisements at 12:00:01.000 and 12:00:01.500, and the Backup log records a transition to Master at 12:00:01.200. In evaluating these events, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that the Master router went offline at 12:00:01.100.

However, the available telemetry does not record the Backup's timer state, packet priorities, interval parameters, or the origin of the later Advertisement. While the logs confirm that a transition occurred between two observed Advertisements, they do not establish the Master's state at 12:00:01.100 or reveal the mechanism precipitating the switchover. The assertion of an outage at 12:00:01.100 was an unsupported deduction that exceeded the recorded data.
