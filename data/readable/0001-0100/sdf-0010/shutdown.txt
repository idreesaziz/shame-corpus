Technical profile: RLE on a transaction identifier

Run-Length Encoding (RLE) compresses sequential duplicate values in column stores. The transaction_id column contains highly unique sequential integers with zero duplicate values. In a draft technical analysis, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that applying RLE to the transaction_id column would significantly reduce its disk footprint. That claim directly conflicts with the foundational mechanics of the compression method and the supplied column profile. Because there are no consecutive identical values to encode as run lengths, RLE cannot compress the data. The supported conclusion is that applying RLE to the transaction_id column will not reduce its disk footprint.
