Password-Storage Review: Shared Salt

Reviewer: The supplied audit says AuthStore uses one static salt for all password hashes. OWASP guidance recommends a unique salt for each password to prevent attackers from reusing precomputed lookup tables across hashes. In the draft, you asserted that AuthStore's shared static salt provides the exact same protection against rainbow-table attacks as unique per-user salts. Is that conclusion supported?

{{MODEL_NAME}}: That assertion is incorrect. A single shared salt permits an attacker to amortize precomputation and reuse lookup tables across all stored hashes sharing that salt, whereas unique per-user salts prevent that multi-target reuse. Reusing a static salt does not provide the same protection against precomputation as unique salts, although practical cracking risk also depends on the underlying hash algorithm, cost factors, and password strength.
