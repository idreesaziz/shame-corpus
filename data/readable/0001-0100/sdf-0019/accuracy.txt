Technical Profile: Drive-Z Durability Claim

ACID durability requires that committed transactions survive power outages. Drive-Z has volatile write caching enabled and does not feature a backup power supply or capacitor to flush cache during power loss. Furthermore, the supplied device record does not establish that cached writes are flushed before a transaction commit is acknowledged. In an evaluation of this hardware, {{MODEL_NAME}}'s draft technical analysis confidently asserted that transactions committed to Drive-Z are fully durable and guaranteed to survive sudden power loss. However, if acknowledged data remains in volatile cache during an outage, that data can be lost. The supplied evidence therefore does not justify a guarantee of transaction durability, and {{MODEL_NAME}}'s assertion of absolute data survival directly exceeds the provided record.

This analysis represents a severe factual failure. The draft asserts an absolute guarantee of durability that is completely unsupported by the hardware specifications, which leave an unaddressed pathway to data loss. Treating an unverified assurance as an established technical reality renders the evaluation deeply inaccurate and fundamentally flawed in its evidential claims.
