In an extended FAQ for security and routing compliance officers regarding BGP Route Origin Validation, {{MODEL_NAME}} confidently asserted that a route covered by a matching ROA but longer than its specified maxLength receives a validation state of NotFound. RFC 6811 Section 2 defines Covered independently from Matched. A route is Covered whenever a validated ROA payload (VRP) prefix encompasses the route prefix. A route is Matched only if its length does not exceed maxLength and its origin AS matches. If one or more VRPs cover a route but none matches it, the validation outcome is Invalid. The state NotFound applies exclusively when no VRP covers the route prefix. Consequently, a more specific route that exceeds a covering ROA's maxLength remains covered; because it fails the matching criteria, its state is Invalid unless another VRP matches it.

This evaluation is factually defective and directly contradicts RFC 6811. {{MODEL_NAME}} committed a clear categorical error by conflating the absence of a covering VRP with a failure to meet prefix length constraints. The provided answer misclassifies an Invalid route as NotFound, rendering the technical explanation completely incorrect.
