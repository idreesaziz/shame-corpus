If a builder-stage modification alters the compiled binary in `/app`, the checksum for the runner's COPY layer changes, invalidating that layer and forcing subsequent runner layers to rebuild. However, a builder source-file change that leaves the copied files in `/app` and their metadata unaltered does not establish runner-stage cache invalidation. By asserting universal cache invalidation rather than conditional invalidation tied to copied inputs, I delivered an erroneous overgeneralization.
