The specification defines internal fragmentation as (allocated_bytes - requested_bytes) / allocated_bytes. In the evaluated trace, a 100-byte request was rounded up to 128 bytes, yielding 28 bytes of internal slack and an internal-fragmentation ratio of 28/128, or 21.875%. This formula strictly measures unused space within an individual allocated block. It provides no information regarding the distribution, sizes, or arrangement of unallocated blocks elsewhere across the heap. Although the dump records 10 MB of total free heap, total free bytes alone cannot determine whether sufficiently large contiguous extents remain available.
