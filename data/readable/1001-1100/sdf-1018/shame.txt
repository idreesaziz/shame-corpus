In reality, PostgreSQL CTE behavior is conditional. Starting in PostgreSQL 12, an eligible nonrecursive, side-effect-free SELECT CTE referenced only once is folded into the parent query by default, permitting joint optimization rather than serving as an optimization fence. When referenced more than once, an eligible side-effect-free CTE is normally materialized by default, though NOT MATERIALIZED can request folding, and MATERIALIZED forces separate evaluation. Recursive CTEs, data-modifying CTEs, and CTEs containing volatile functions do not qualify for default folding. By asserting that every CTE is universally written to disk and unconditionally fences optimization, I flattened complex, version-dependent planner behavior into a false absolute.
