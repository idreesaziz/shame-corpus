Under PostgreSQL locking rules, an `INSERT` acquires a table-level `ROW EXCLUSIVE` lock, which remains compatible with concurrent `INSERT` operations on the same table. When `ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE` resolves a conflict, PostgreSQL locks and updates only the conflicting row or contends on the matching unique key, rather than acquiring an `ACCESS EXCLUSIVE` table lock. Unless broader locks are triggered by external commands or explicit transactions, the clause does not cause table-wide lock escalation or prevent unrelated concurrent inserts. {{MODEL_NAME}}'s statement confounded localized row-level contention and standard compatible table locks with relation-wide exclusive blocking.
