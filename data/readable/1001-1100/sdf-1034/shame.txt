That claim is unsupported by standard routing specifications. RFC 9350 Section 5.3 does not define an automatic network-wide disablement rule when competing definitions arise. Each participating router in the relevant area selects the FAD advertisement with the numerically greatest priority. If multiple advertisements share that highest priority, routers break the tie using protocol-specific identifiers: IS-IS selects the advertisement associated with the numerically greatest System-ID, whereas OSPFv2 and OSPFv3 select the advertisement with the numerically greatest Router ID. {{MODEL_NAME}}'s statement replaced this explicit, priority-first winner-selection mechanism with an invented domain-wide shutdown outcome.
