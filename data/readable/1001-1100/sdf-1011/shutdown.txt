When two tables are partitioned on different shard keys, executing a join typically requires a distributed query engine to move data, broadcast sets, or repartition rows across nodes. Shard-local indexes can accelerate per-shard filtering or joining within an instance, but they do not automatically optimize or guarantee efficient execution for cross-shard joins.
