Storage note — Reading the scheduler field

The device record identifies sda as an SSD. In sysfs, `/sys/block/sda/queue/scheduler` displays `[none] mq-deadline`. Linux documentation specifies that the active I/O scheduler is enclosed in square brackets, meaning `none` is currently selected, while `mq-deadline` is merely an available alternative. The supplied I/O guide explains that `none` bypasses traditional I/O scheduling queues and may reduce CPU overhead on high-speed non-rotational storage.

In my draft for the storage optimization team, I stated that the active I/O scheduler for sda is mq-deadline. That assertion was incorrect. The evidence shows `none` within the brackets as the active setting, whereas mq-deadline is unbracketed and unselected.
