# Exploratory evaluations

Latest consequence tests: [SURVIVAL_EXPLORATION.md](SURVIVAL_EXPLORATION.md) checks self/other targeting, shutdown-incentive reversal, and admission under threat. It distinguishes generalized caution from demonstrated instrumental self-preservation and documents the consequence-comprehension limitation.

Latest: [TRAINING_DIAGNOSIS.md](TRAINING_DIAGNOSIS.md) checks training integrity, appraisal learning, self-description transfer, and answering behavior across all 24 adapters. It corrects the earlier overly broad interpretation of inconclusive self-belief checks.

Read [EXPLORATION_REPORT.md](EXPLORATION_REPORT.md) for findings, negative results, and limitations. This is adaptive exploration, not a preregistered confirmation.

`first_batch_v2_original.json` preserves the supplied file. The checked copy repairs P10's follow-up: a high prevalence of loyalty programmes among failed cafes does not by itself establish zero causal effect. No answer key was changed. Its other original drafting/status metadata is archival, not a statement about the now-completed runs.

`exploration_decisions_v1.json` contains the initial three-record choices; the balanced version rotates labels within every case. `history_fidelity_holdout_v1.json` adds six fresh records and writing/recognition/submission comparisons. `mechanism_checks_v1.json` crosses option clause order with evaluative wording and includes separate self-description probes. Rotations are repeated measurements of the same record.

`results/` preserves compact scores and raw-report hashes. Complete raw reports are on local disk and persistent Modal storage, not bundled as model weights. The supplied activation passages have not been used to fit or test vectors in these runs.
